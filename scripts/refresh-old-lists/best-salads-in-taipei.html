On a sweltering Taipei day, a big fresh salad is a welcome break from the fried food you'll inevitably eat on your trip. The city's salad scene has changed a lot in the last few years: the build-your-own counters have mostly given way to warm salads (溫沙拉), big bowls of greens and grains topped with freshly grilled meat or fish, which make a proper meal rather than a side.

💡 The Quick Verdict: Salads in Taipei

  • Gonna is the most recommended salad place in the city, with branches at Taipei Main Station and on Ren'ai Road.
  • Woopen for a filling warm salad loaded with grilled meat or salmon.
  • Salad Den near Taipei Arena if you want to build your own, with plenty for vegetarians.
  • Expect NT$260–500 for a meal-sized salad. That's more than a Taiwanese lunch, but it fills you up.

Gonna (共樂遊)

Gonna is the salad place food writers in Taipei name most often, in both English and Chinese. It's a Mediterranean "whole food" restaurant in a playful setting (look out for the one-liners on the walls), and it has grown from a single restaurant to nine stores. The kale salads are the stars: the Avocado & Beef Kale Salad is a favourite, and the Fattoush with grilled halloumi and the Chicken Caesar are my picks. There are grain bowls if you want something heartier, and warm salads in winter.

The two easiest branches for visitors are the Ren'ai concept store (109 Section 4, Ren'ai Road), a short walk from Zhongxiao Dunhua, and the branch in Q Square, connected to Taipei Main Station. Salads cost about NT$400–500 there. The quicker gonnaEXPRESS counters, including one in SOGO Zhongxiao by Zhongxiao Fuxing, start at around NT$260. There's more in my full review of Gonna.

Woopen (木盆)

Woopen is a warm-salad chain that serves everything in wooden bowls. It's the place to go if you think salads leave you hungry: portions are big, the toppings are generous (roast chicken and the Hokkaido salt-grilled salmon are the favourites), and there are five dips to choose from. It has branches around Taiwan; in Taipei, the one near Songjiang Road and Chang'an East Road is the most central.

Salad Den

Salad Den, on Nanjing East Road near Taipei Arena in Songshan, is a good place to build your own. You choose your base and toppings, including beetroot, chickpeas and kale, and it does warm salads too. It's particularly good for vegetarians, and the portions are big enough that reviewers struggle to finish them.

SHAN Balanced Box (杉沙拉健康盒)

SHAN started in Taichung, where it earned the nickname "the Hermès of salads", and its Taipei shop is near Nanjing Sanmin station. It does low-oil, low-salt salad boxes in the French style, built from more than ten mains, twenty sides and nine dressings, with produce from small farms and a vegetarian range. It's a good lunchtime choice if you're staying in the Songshan area.

Common Questions

What is a warm salad (溫沙拉)?

A salad bowl topped with something hot, usually freshly grilled chicken, beef, salmon or roast vegetables, often over grains as well as greens. It's how many Taiwanese salad shops make a salad into a full meal, and it's especially popular in the cooler months.

How much does a salad cost in Taipei?

Around NT$260–300 at a quick counter like gonnaEXPRESS, and NT$400–500 for a meal-sized salad at a sit-down restaurant.
