Looking for food that's good for you without being joyless? Taipei has a handful of restaurants that do healthy properly: seasonal grain bowls, dietitian-designed brunches and plant-based cooking that even committed meat-eaters order again. These are the ones worth crossing town for, and how to get a table.

💡 The Quick Verdict: Healthy Food in Taipei

  • Sugar Pea for the best seasonal bowls in the city. Book, because walk-ins are often turned away at lunch.
  • Tamed Fox for a healthy brunch, from a menu designed by a former dietitian, with branches in Da'an and Xinyi.
  • Miacucina for vegetarian Buddha bowls, with branches by Zhongshan, Zhongxiao Fuxing and Taipei 101.
  • Ooh Cha Cha for cheap vegan bowls and burgers.
  • Expect NT$400–650 for a main at the sit-down places, plus 10% service at some.

Sugar Pea Cafe & Kitchen

Its tagline is "Wholesome, Seasonal, Simple Cuisine", and Sugar Pea lives up to it. The menu is built on seasonal salads and bowls with rice, quinoa, grains or legumes as their base, and the dishes are healthy and fresh without ever tasting like a compromise. The salmon green rice bowl and the avocado and shrimp salad are the regulars' picks, and my Korean pork bowl was excellent. Bowls and salads run from about NT$450 to NT$650, and there's a 10% service charge.

It's in a quiet lane off Ren'ai Road, about ten minutes' walk from Xinyi Anhe station, and it's popular enough to have been part of Taipei Restaurant Week in 2026. Book ahead, especially for lunch. There's more in my full review of Sugar Pea.

Address: 16, Alley 20, Lane 300, Section 4, Ren'ai Road, Da'an (仁愛路四段300巷20弄16號)

Tamed Fox

Tamed Fox was started by a former dietitian, and its whole menu is low in sugar and fat and high in fibre, with wholemeal baking. It's still one of the best brunches in Taipei. The acai and Greek yoghurt bowl, the pan-fried chicken bowl on quinoa, the avocado open sandwich and the whole-wheat cinnamon roll are the dishes reviewers mention most. There are good vegetarian options, and it's pet-friendly.

The original branch is in Da'an, about five minutes from Daan station, and a larger second branch, with an outdoor courtyard, is on Songren Road in Xinyi. Both open from 09:00 to 17:30. There's more in my full review of Tamed Fox.

Da'an: 5, Lane 76, Siwei Road (四維路76巷5號)  ·  Xinyi: B1, 91 Songren Road (松仁路91號B1)

Miacucina

Miacucina is an all-vegetarian restaurant chain with one of the biggest menus in Taipei, and its Buddha bowls are the healthiest way through it: the Super Protein Buddha Bowl and the Green Giant Buddha Bowl are both around NT$470–500. There are big salads, paninis and pastas too, and the vegan choices are more limited than the vegetarian ones. Meat-eaters won't miss the meat.

There are four branches in Taipei City: on Fuxing South Road near Zhongxiao Fuxing, in Shin Kong Mitsukoshi A11 by Taipei 101, in Shin Kong Mitsukoshi Nanxi by Zhongshan station, and in Tianmu, closest to Zhishan station. At peak times, the mall branches limit tables to two hours and Fuxing to 90 minutes. There's more in my full review of Miacucina.

Ooh Cha Cha

Started by Californian and Taiwanese friends, Ooh Cha Cha is an all-vegan cafe serving grain bowls (multigrain rice or quinoa, with tempeh and plenty of vegetables), burgers and sandwiches, at some of the best prices for Western-style food in the city. The original branch is near Guting station. The same team opened a second vegan restaurant, Sunami, in 2025. There's more in my full review of Ooh Cha Cha.

Common Questions

Is Taiwanese food healthy?

It can be. Plenty of everyday Taiwanese food is steamed, boiled or soup-based, with lots of vegetables and tofu, but night-market food leans fried and sweet, and many drinks are loaded with sugar. Ask for 無糖 (no sugar) or 微糖 (a little sugar) when you order bubble tea or juice.

What is 健康餐 (healthy meal) in Taiwan?

A whole category of lunch-box shops selling calorie-counted boxes of grilled protein, vegetables and brown rice, aimed at office workers and gym-goers. They're cheaper than the restaurants above, usually NT$100–200, and you'll find them around most office areas.

Where can I eat healthy near Taipei 101?

Miacucina in Shin Kong Mitsukoshi A11 is a few minutes' walk away, and Tamed Fox's Xinyi branch on Songren Road is close by.
