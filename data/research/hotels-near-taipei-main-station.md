# Research: Hotels near Taipei Main Station

Research date: 27 September 2026. This is research only, not article copy.

## Method and caveats

- **Distinct publisher domains only.** Booking engines and aggregators (Booking, Agoda, Expedia, Hotels.com, Trip.com, Klook, KKday, TripAdvisor, Kayak, Trivago, Hostelworld, Wanderlog, and the many "xxx.taiwanhotelstaipei.com" or "gettaipeihotels.com" mirror sites) are **not counted**. I used them only to check addresses and open status.
- **Taiwan Obsessed and Nickkembel Travels have the same author (Nick Kembel)**, confirmed on [taiwanobsessed.com/about](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/about/) and [nickkembel.com/about-me](https://www.nickkembel.com/about-me/). I count them as **ONE publisher**, shown as "TO/NK". Counting them separately would double-count one person's opinion.
- **Lonely Planet "a/lod/" pages** (e.g. Taipei M Hotel) are booking-feed listings, not editorial, so I don't count them. LP "poi-sle" pages are editorial, but the Flip Flop Garden one now returns 404, so I don't count it either.
- **Michelin Guide "hotels-stays"** pages count, as the brief says. They are the old Tablet Hotels selection, so they are curated but also bookable.
- **Points and travel-review blogs** with first-hand stays (Travel Codex, Head for Points, etc.) count as independent editorial.
- Where I didn't open a source myself and am relying on the search-engine snippet, I say "(snippet)".
- Walking times are approximate. The station is a huge complex, and the walk from the Airport MRT (A1) platforms to the east-side hotels adds 5+ minutes.
- The Time Out Taipei "best hotels" list ([timeout.com](https://www.timeout.com/taipei/hotels/best-hotels-in-taipei), dated Sept 2024) has only one hotel near the station: Hotel Resonance. No Condé Nast Traveller, Telegraph, Guardian, T+L, CNN Travel or Forbes Travel Guide coverage turned up for any hotel in the station area. Forbes's Taipei list ([forbestravelguide.com](https://www.forbestravelguide.com/destinations/taipei-taiwan)) has no hotel near the station.

## Candidate hotels (20)

### UPSCALE

#### 1. Palais de Chine Hotel (君品酒店)
- **Brand:** LDC Hotels & Resorts (independent Taiwanese group). Preferred Hotels member.
- **Address:** No. 3, Sec. 1, Chengde Rd, Datong District (above Q Square / Taipei Bus Station).
- **Walk to station:** 0-3 min. It is directly connected underground to Taipei Main Station and Q Square. Taiwanderers says it is next to exit Y5. Taiwan Obsessed (July 2026 stay) warns the entrance is "surprisingly difficult to find" from the underground passages. It is one of the closest upscale options to the Airport MRT (A1).
- **Price band:** Upscale. No reliable NT$ figure found (unverified).
- **Why pick it:** The most-cited luxury pick at the station. Neo-classical European interiors, 286 rooms, some with balconies. The three-Michelin-star Cantonese restaurant Le Palais is in-house.
- **Sources: 5 sources:**
  - guide.michelin.com ([link](https://guide.michelin.com/us/en/hotels-stays/taipei/palais-de-chine-7560))
  - TO/NK: first-hand July 2026 ([review](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/palais-de-chine-taipei-review/), [area guide](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taipei-main-station-hotels/), [nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/where-to-stay-in-taipei/))
  - taiwanderers.com ([link](https://taiwanderers.com/taipei-main-station-hotels/))
  - travelcodex.com: first-hand Jan 2024, mixed verdict ([link](https://www.travelcodex.com/review-palais-de-chine-hotel-taipei/))
  - twobytheworld.com ([link](https://twobytheworld.com/where-to-stay-in-taipei-taiwan/))
- **Open status:** OPEN. The Michelin listing is live with a booking calendar, and Taiwan Obsessed stayed in July 2026.
- **Traveller notes:**
  - No swimming pool on site. Taiwanderers mentions free access to a pool nearby.
  - The interiors are dark, which not everyone likes.
  - Travel Codex found the standard room compact, though the bathroom is big.
  - Taiwan Obsessed notes an executive lounge (Le Salon).

## 1. Ranked shortlist (distinct editorial domains, then fit for luggage-carrying Airport MRT arrivals)

**Upscale**
1. **Palais de Chine**: 5 sources. Directly connected to the station and close to A1.
2. **Hotel Resonance (Tapestry by Hilton)**: 6 sources. Ranked below Palais only because it's a 10-ish minute walk / one MRT stop.
3. **Sheraton Grand Taipei**: 4 sources. Also about 10 min or one stop east.
4. **Caesar Park Taipei**: 2 sources. Unbeatable location, but the rooms are dated.

## 3. Station-area facts for the intro (with sources)

- **The Airport MRT terminal (A1) is a separate station**, about 250 m west of the main Taipei Station building. A 115 m underground walkway connects the two, and another passage runs about 200 m west to Beimen MRT. A1 opened for commercial service on 2 March 2017. [Wikipedia: Taipei Main Station (Taoyuan Metro)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_Main_Station_(Taoyuan_Metro))
- **Journey time:** Express trains reach Taoyuan Airport in 35-37 minutes depending on the terminal, running every 15 minutes. The published fare is NT$160, with a long-running NT$10 discount making it NT$150. [Wikipedia: Taoyuan Airport MRT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoyuan_Airport_MRT) (re-check the fare before publishing)
- **In-town check-in at A1 (B1 level):**
  - Open 06:00-21:30. Check in at least 3 hours before departure, for same-day flights from Taoyuan.
  - Participating airlines: China Airlines/Mandarin, EVA Air/Uni Air, Cathay Pacific and STARLUX.
  - Source: [Taoyuan Metro official ITCI page](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_pages/checkin/index.php)
- **A1 has 7 exits**, mostly along Civic Boulevard and Chongqing N. Rd, with YouBike stations outside exits 6 and 7. [Taoyuan Metro A1 guide](https://www.tymetro.com.tw/tymetro-new/en/_pages/travel-guide/A1)
- **The station has three linked complexes:** the Airport MRT (A1), the TRA/HSR railway station, and Taipei Main MRT (Red and Blue lines). Taiwan Obsessed calls the underground malls and tunnels between them "a maze". [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taipei-main-station-hotels/)
- **Underground malls and exit letters:**
  - Y exits = Taipei City Mall, running northwest. It is about 825 m long with 28 exits and leads toward A1, Star Hostel and Old Door.
  - Z exits (plus M3-M8) = Station Front Metro Mall, opening onto Zhongxiao W. Rd, where Caesar Park, Cosmos and Meworld are.
  - K = K Underground Mall, south side.
  - R exits = Zhongshan Underground Street, running north.
  - Sources: [Taiwanderers](https://taiwanderers.com/taipei-city-mall-underground-mall/), [Wikipedia: Taipei City Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taipei_City_Mall), [Wikipedia: Station Front Metro Mall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Station_Front_Metro_Mall)
- **Luggage lockers** (all from [Taiwan Obsessed luggage guide](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/luggage-storage-lockers-taipei-taoyuan-taiwan/)):
  - Main station, B1: coin/EasyCard lockers near the TRA/HSR gates, about NT$10/hour, 24-hour max. Small 43×60×42 cm, large 56×90×42 cm.
  - A1: orange OWLocker lockers at NT$40 (small) or NT$60 (large) per 3 hours, up to 72 hours.
  - Taipei Station Baggage Service Center, one block east: 08:00-20:00, NT$30/50/70 per day by bag size.
  - Tip: photograph your locker number, because the B1 level is confusing.
- **Area pitch:** the area south of the station has the biggest cluster of hotels and restaurants and is closest to Ximending. The north side (Huayin St / Taiyuan Rd) is where the hostels cluster, near the Airport MRT, Ningxia Night Market and Dihua St. [Taiwan Obsessed](https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/taipei-main-station-hotels/), [Nickkembel](https://www.nickkembel.com/where-to-stay-in-taipei/)
- **Taxis:** there is a taxi pick-up on B1 of the Airport MRT station (Taiwan Obsessed, above). Unverified with the operator.
