# Research: Taipei Hotels With a Pool

Checked 3 October 2026. This file is research, not article copy. Proposed slug: `taipei-hotels-with-pools`.

**Scope used**
- Hotels inside Taipei City with a swimming pool guests can actually swim in: rooftop, infinity, outdoor heated, indoor, family and two or three Beitou hot spring hotels with swimsuit pools for contrast.
- One New Taipei hotel (Hilton Taipei Sinban, Banqiao) is included as a "just over the river" extra, because two Taiwanese round-ups name it as the area's best infinity pool.
- Private in-room plunge pools and nude hot spring baths are not "pools" for this page; the Beitou guide covers them.

**How this was checked**
- **Two passes**, as for the earlier stay guides.
  - Pass 1: consensus count of English and Traditional Chinese editorial pages (opened, not snippets). Anything taken from a search snippet only is marked "(s)".
  - Pass 2: every hotel's pool checked against its **official pool or facilities page** (opened in the browser on 3 Oct 2026), then dated reviews where the official page was silent.
  - Marriott-group pages (W, Renaissance, Marriott, Aloft) were read from the "Experiences" pool panels on marriott.com. hyatt.com and hilton.com still block automated access, so the Grand Hyatt and Hilton Sinban facts rest on earlier research and snippets (flagged).
- **Excluded from counts:** booking engines and OTA content (Booking, Agoda, Trip.com, Klook, KKday, Expedia, Travelocity, Hotels.com, funtime.com.tw, colatour, Wing On), TripAdvisor, Wanderlog, Mr & Mrs Smith (booking club), and pure affiliate/aggregator lists with no named author: theluxuryeditor.com, hotelierschoice.com, staygenie.app, taipei-best-hotels.com, besthotels-taipei.com, allhotelswithpool.com, gettaipeihotels.com, outdoorproject.com user list. Nick Kembel = taiwanobsessed.com + nickkembel.com (one publisher). hk01 is counted once even though two hk01 articles were opened.
- **Prices.** Booking.com, TWD, 2 adults, 1 room, **Wed 11 to Thu 12 Nov 2026** and **Sat 14 to Sun 15 Nov 2026**, lowest price on the page, pulled on **3 Oct 2026** (re-pulled for every hotel, so some differ slightly from the 29 Sep figures in the area guides). Where Saturday showed no availability, Sat 21 Nov was tried. Single-source snapshot, not editorial evidence.
- **Walks.** Area-guide figures are reused (Google Maps runs of 29 Sep 2026, exit-level). New hotels were run on Google Maps on 3 Oct 2026 **from the station, not a named exit** (Google resolves exit names to the station point), so treat them as ±2 min.
- **Klook.** Every Klook URL below was opened (hotel detail page) on 3 Oct 2026 or in the earlier area research, and the street address checked against the official one.
- **Working notes:** scratchpad `pools/owner-pool-mentions.txt` (every pool sentence in the owner's posts).

## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **Only a handful of Taipei hotel pools sell entry to non-guests, and the Shangri-La is the clearest.** Its official health-club page sells a single entry to the heated 43F rooftop pool for **NT$2,000 (12+) or NT$1,000 (under 12, with an adult)**. Hotel guests' friends pay NT$1,000 weekdays / NT$1,500 weekends. Humble House, Caesar Metro, Proverbs, Gloria Residence and Le Méridien say outright that their pools are for in-house guests (or members) only.
2. **Swim caps are mandatory at most hotel pools, not just public ones.** The official pages of **Humble House, the Okura, Miramar Garden, Caesar Metro and Gloria Residence** all require (or "ask you to wear") a cap. So do all of Beitou's mixed swimsuit pools and Taipei's public pools. Pack one.
3. **Winter closures are common and not always obvious:**
   - **Humble House:** 7F outdoor pool **closed November to March** (official).
   - **Sheraton Grand:** 18F rooftop pool **closed January and February** (official).
   - **Caesar Metro:** 8F outdoor pool **open March to December only, and unheated** (official).
   - **Grand Mayfull:** heated, but shut for annual maintenance (19 Jan – 13 Feb in 2026) and on the 2nd Monday of each month 15:00–22:00 (official).
   - **Renaissance Shihlin:** the rooftop infinity pool is reported **closed in winter** with shorter spring/autumn hours (hk01, May 2023; yam). The months are not on the Marriott page (gap).
   - **The Grand Hotel:** the 50 m pool stays open all year (shorter winter hours), but the **children's pool closes November to April** (official).
   - **Shangri-La:** the 43F rooftop pool is open all year apart from its annual maintenance month (31 Aug – 30 Sep in 2026); the second pool on 7F is summer-only.
4. **The Grand Hotel has a 50 m Olympic-size outdoor pool (25 × 50 m, 1.2–5 m deep) plus a kids' pool**, run by the Yuan Shan Club next door. Hotel guests use it free from 12:00 on arrival day to 11:00 on departure day. It is the biggest hotel pool in the city by far. Whether it is heated is not stated officially (an old swimmersguide listing says unheated, about 20 °C in winter – unconfirmed).
5. **Renaissance Taipei Shihlin is the only true rooftop *infinity* pool in Taipei City** (25 m, 1.0–1.5 m deep, facing Yangmingshan). Five Taiwanese publishers single it out. Marriott's page: 07:00–12:00 and 14:00–21:00 daily, lifeguard, infinity edge.
6. **Cheaper hotels with pools exist but are rare:** **Caesar Metro** (Wanhua, NT$3,330 on a November Wednesday), **Miramar Garden** (NT$5,444, heated outdoor pool, cap required) and **Parkview** (NT$6,200, rooftop pool garden). Hilton Taipei Sinban (Banqiao) is NT$5,890 for a 32F infinity pool.
7. **Caesar Metro's weekend price on Booking is strange.** Every November Saturday tried (7, 14, 21) showed only rooms from NT$15,400–17,600, against NT$3,330 midweek. Treat the weekend figure as unreliable (gap).
8. **Not to use / closed:**
   - **Howard Plaza (福華):** the official Taipei facilities page (3 Oct 2026) **no longer lists a pool at all**. It lists only the gym moved to room 836 (from 1 Mar 2026), the shopping arcade, a closed kids' room and suspended EV chargers. The Daan research's "pool closed all of 2026" snippet is consistent with this.
   - **Hotel Royal Nikko Taipei (台北老爺):** a 2018 SETN list gives it a 13F outdoor pool, but the **current official facilities page lists no pool** (B1 lounge, 4F spa room, parking and shops only). Don't include.
   - **Aloft Taipei Zhongshan:** hk01 (Feb 2026) says it has a top-floor infinity pool; **marriott.com lists no pool** for it. Don't include.
   - **Fraser Residence Taipei (Beitou)**, with a heated pool, is not due until 2027.
9. **Hours conflict at the Grand Hyatt.** The 101 research gives the 5F outdoor heated pool as 06:00–22:30; Hyatt's Club Oasis page (search snippet, site blocked) says **06:00–21:30**, and lists wet-area maintenance Mondays in 2026 including **2 November**. Say "early morning to late evening" or use 21:30.
10. **W Taipei's WET pool hours are now settled:** marriott.com says **06:00–22:00 daily, heated, outdoor, swim-up bar, lifeguard** (resolves the 101 research's "06:00–22:00 vs 24h" conflict).
11. **Tattoos:** none of the eleven official pool pages read (Shangri-La, Mandarin, Humble House, Okura, Le Méridien, Sheraton Grand, Grand Hotel, Grand Mayfull, Miramar, Caesar Metro, Marriott-group panels) mentions tattoos. Unlike Japan, no published ban was found. Say so, without promising.
12. **The owner's Daan guide is now out of date on the Shangri-La** – it still says the pool "reopens on 1 October 2026" (two places). See section 7.
13. **Public pools:** Taipei's district sports centres charge about **NT$110** a swim (adult). The Dahu Park and Yuquan Park public pools had their operator walk out on 13 Oct 2025 (PTS, 8 Oct 2025); the city said it would take over and reopen them in early November 2025. Current status not re-checked (the owner's `dahu-park` post mentions the pool).

## 5. General facts

### 5.1 Swim caps
- **Most Taipei pools require a cap.** Verified on official pages: Humble House, Okura ("請配戴泳帽"), Miramar Garden, Caesar Metro, Gloria Residence; all Beitou mixed swimsuit pools (Spring City, Grand View outdoor, Asia Pacific); Taipei City public pools ("換穿游泳衣褲戴泳帽", laws.gov.taipei 溫水游泳池使用須知).
- Not stated on: Shangri-La, Mandarin, W, Marriott, Renaissance, Sheraton Grand, Grand Mayfull, Le Méridien, Grand Hotel pages. Don't claim either way for those.
- Whether hotels sell or lend caps at the pool: **not found** on any official page. Advise packing one.

### 5.2 Winter closures and maintenance (Nov 2026 relevance in bold)
- **Humble House: closed Nov–Mar.**
- Sheraton Grand: closed Jan–Feb.
- Caesar Metro: closed Jan–Feb (open Mar–Dec), unheated.
- **Renaissance Shihlin: reported closed in winter** (months unconfirmed).
