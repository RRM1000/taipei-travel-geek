## 1. Pingxi line one-day pass

- **Official name:** 平溪線/深澳雙支線一日週遊券 (Pingxi Line / Shen'ao Line One-Day Pass). Valid on the Shen'ao and Pingxi lines, **Badouzi – Ruifang – Jingtong**, unlimited hop-on hop-off, on the day of sale only; must be date-stamped; non-refundable.
- **Price:** **NT$120 adult, NT$60 concession**. The TRA page says the price changed to these figures on **23 June 2025** (114年6月23日).
- **Sold at:** Banqiao, Taipei, Songshan, Keelung, Badu, Ruifang, Houtong, Yilan, Luodong, Pingxi, Jingtong, Shifen.
- **Still sold?** Yes: listed on the TRA tourist-tickets page, page generated 27 Oct 2026, site updated 25 Oct 2026.
- Source: railway.gov.tw > 旅遊票券 > 周遊券/悠遊券 > 平溪深澳一日週遊券 > 全票 detail (ticketNo 0603), opened 27 Oct 2026. https://www.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip003/tip313/view10/sort?ticketSortNo=0004
- **Conflicts:** linego.me (5 Oct 2026) and many English blogs still say NT$80; the site's own `/taiwan-railway#pingxi` says "about NT$52". Both are out of date. **Flag: /taiwan-railway needs correcting** (not touched here; posts.json is off limits for this task).

## 2. Fares and timetable (TRA timetable query, opened 27 Oct 2026)

Query dates: Wed 4 Nov 2026 and Sat 7 Nov 2026. tip.railway.gov.tw/tra-tip-web/tip/tip001/tip112/gobytime

**Fares (adult, Local train):**
- Taipei – Ruifang: NT$73 (Local, 46–57 min; Fast Local about 48 min; faster reserved trains about 35–40 min)
- Ruifang – Shifen: NT$29, 27–33 min
- Ruifang – Jingtong: NT$44, 46–61 min
- Shifen – Pingxi: NT$22, about 13 min
- Pingxi – Jingtong: NT$22, about 5 min
- Classic loop Ruifang > Shifen > Pingxi > Jingtong > Ruifang = 29 + 22 + 22 + 44 = **NT$117**, i.e. less than the NT$120 pass. The pass only saves money with extra stops (Houtong, Wanggu, Lingjiao, Dahua) or an out-and-back.

## 3. Bus 795 (Taiwan Tourist Shuttle Muzha–Pingxi route)

- Starts at **MRT Muzha station** (Brown line), via Shenkeng, Shiding, Jingtong and Pingxi to Shifen (smiletaiwan.cw.com.tw article 6235 (s); NOWnews 6789883, opened).
- Fare **NT$45 one way**, Muzha to Shifen **about 1 hour** (NOWnews, Feb 2026, opened).
- Three-section fare; operator Taipei Bus Co. (臺北客運). Timetable on Bus+ (data updated 27 Oct 2026): weekday departures from 06:00 to 23:20, roughly hourly through the middle of the day with more in the late afternoon (busplus.app/xinbei/bus/16719, via WebFetch). Direction labelling on that page is unclear, so the article gives no exact times.
- Older guide (s): about hourly on weekdays, about every half hour at weekends.

## 4. Shifen

**Shifen Waterfall** (New Taipei City Travel official page, attraction 111592, updated 17 Aug 2026, opened):
- Hours: **October–May 09:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30); June–September 09:00–18:00 (last entry 17:30)**. Open daily except **Lunar New Year's Eve**.
- Tel 02-2495-8605; No. 10 Qiankeng, Pingxi.
- Ledge (cascade) waterfall on the Keelung River, called "Taiwan's Niagara"; rainbow over the pool in sun ("Rainbow Pool"); Yanjingdong (Eyeglass Cave) Waterfall and potholes along the way from the Shifen Visitor Center via Siguangtan Bridge; several viewing platforms.
- Size: about 40 m wide, about 20 m drop (PChome/Taiyang, 25 Oct 2026 (s)).
- **Free entry:** New Taipei press release (2023) and EDH (s); the official page lists no fee.
- Walk: about 15 min from the Shifen Visitor Center on the "friendly trail" (New Taipei press release (s)); 20–30 min from the station (blogs (s)). Route via the **Siguangtan** and **Guanpu** suspension bridges behind the visitor centre; Guanpu is where the Keelung River meets the Yuetaoliao stream, and trains are often seen crossing (travel.yam.com (s)).

**Shifen Old Street** (official page 111989, updated 25 Jul 2026): the train runs between the shopfronts; lantern shops open weekdays and holidays; "don't cross the tracks at random". **Shifen Station** (page 111590): largest station on the line; Pingxi line built for coal by Yen Yun-lian, opened 1921, nationalised 1929, saved from closure and turned into a tourist line in 1992.

**Sky lantern prices:**
- PTS News, 4 Apr 2025 (opened): **4-colour NT$250** (up from NT$200 in 2024, NT$100 earlier), **8-colour NT$350** (unchanged); prices are a recommended price set by the business association; shopkeepers expect another rise if New Taipei's draft *sky lantern sustainability ordinance* brings in a levy.
- Single colour: about **NT$200** in a 2026 guide; older guides NT$150 (gofunit.com 2026 (s); others (s)). **Unconfirmed**; written as "about NT$150–200".
- No official price list found.

**Lanterns on the tracks: the law (udn/United Daily News, 30 Jul 2026, opened):**
- Railway Act art. 57 bars walking onto the line; fines **NT$10,000–50,000** (Railway Bureau, via udn 9657217 (s) and 9659381).
- Over 100 fines in five years, 60+ of them on the Pingxi line.
- A trial let people launch lanterns on the track at Shifen, Pingxi and Jingtong at weekends and New Year, 08:00–20:00, from late Nov 2025 to 4 Jan 2026, while trains were suspended (CNA 28 Nov 2025 (s); udn).
- Jan 2026: art. 57 amended to "banned in principle, allowed by exception"; TRA rules of 28 Jan 2026 accept applications only from bodies such as the city or district office, 30 days ahead, with an event name; **no applications as of July 2026**; launching on the track is still an offence for visitors.
- New Taipei drafted rules limiting launches to the area from the Shifen Visitor Center to Shigong Bridge and within 200 m of County Road 106 along the river; "still banned on the track".
- Nothing newer found for Aug–Oct 2026 (search).

**Litter and recycling:** Pingxi District Cleaning Team runs a sky-lantern-paper exchange (5 sheets = a 14-litre rubbish bag, 10 = toilet roll, 50 = washing-up liquid), plus shops crediting NT$1 a sheet (recycle.ntpc.gov.tw category24, via WebFetch; the scheme listed for 2025; a Sept 2026 notice (s) shows it still running on Wednesdays).

## 5. Pingxi and Jingtong

- **Pingxi Old Street** (official page 111603): along a hillside with the railway on a high bridge over the street; *You Are the Apple of My Eye* lantern scene filmed here; Japanese-era air-raid shelters and alarm bell; Eight Spirits Cave beside Guanyin Temple; the tourist board calls its post box Taiwan's oldest (attributed).
- **Jingtong:** terminus; station opened 1929, listed as a historic monument in 2003 (Bella / The News Lens (s)); one of few working Japanese-style wooden stations; bamboo wishing tubes hung along the old street and railway (udn blog 2013 (s); New Taipei press release Feb 2026 (s) mentions wooden station, Japanese dormitories, Jingtong Mining Life Museum, wishing bamboo tubes, miner's lunch boxes).
- **Jingtong Electronic Sky Lantern Hall** (official page 112542, updated Dec 2022): LED "Police Wish Sky Lantern" launches, NT$150, bought at the Pingxi police station, three times an hour, 16:00–19:00 weekdays, 16:00–20:00 holidays. Old page; hedged in copy.

## 6. Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival

- **2027: not announced** as of 27 Oct 2026 (searches in Chinese and English; nothing on newtaipei.travel news).
- Lantern Festival (15th day of the first lunar month) falls on **Saturday 20 February 2027** (wannianli.tianqi.com (s); consistent with Lunar New Year on 6 Feb 2027). The festival is held around this date, usually as two evenings (Pingxi and Shifen).
- 2026 for reference (NOWnews 6789883, opened): **27 Feb** at Pingxi Junior High School and **3 Mar** at Shifen Square, from 18:00; nine waves of 100 (Pingxi) and 150 (Shifen) lanterns; free launch tickets handed out from 10:30 on the day (online booking full); shuttle buses from Taipei Zoo, Shiding interchange, Ruifang station, Keelung station, Shuangxi station; road closures from 16:00. 795 ran but skipped some stops.
- 2025: 8 Feb (Pingxi) and 12 Feb (Shifen) (search (s)).

## 8. Best time / weather

- Weekdays; early trains. PTS (Apr 2025): crowds at weekends and holidays; when the north-east monsoon arrives, visitor numbers on the street drop to 20–30% of a normal weekday (monsoon = cool drizzle, Oct–Mar).
- Waterfall summer hours longer (Jun–Sep).
- Lanterns: heavy rain stops them flying (utravel (s)); light drizzle is OK (s). Copy says only "heavy rain".
