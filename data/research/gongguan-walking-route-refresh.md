## Issues found on other pages (not edited)

Mentions "Road Castle"; OSM marks the old water play park 水悟空樂園 as closed, and the official site now calls the summer area 親水樂園.
- `/treasure-hill`: hours 11:00–21:00; official is 11:00–22:00 outdoors, 11:00–18:00 indoors.
- `/yong-he-soy-milk-king`: "open 24 hours" conflicts with the June 2026 report (05:00–14:00, closed Mondays).
- `/michelin-food-stands-at-night-markets` (#Gongguan) and `/lan-jia-traditional-taiwanese-snack`: Lan Chia Guabao is not on the 2026 Bib Gourmand list.
- `/national-taiwan-university`: 115 ha vs the visitor centre's 110 ha.
- `/the-diner`: one inline.app booking link returns 404.
