# Research: Hotels with a Taipei 101 View for New Year's Eve

Checked **3 October 2026**. This file is research, not article copy. Proposed slug: `taipei-101-view-hotels-new-years-eve`. The NYE in question is **Thursday 31 December 2026**. 1 January 2027 is a Friday, so the New Year holiday runs **Fri 1 to Sun 3 January 2027** (cpok.tw calendar).

**How this was checked**
- **Sources.** Hotel packages come from the hotels' own pages wherever we could open them: Taipei Marriott, Le Méridien, Humble House, Shangri-La, United Hotel and eslite. Where we couldn't, they come from dated Taiwanese news reports, mostly September 2026.
  - hyatt.com, marriott.com and the W's own site would not give package pages to a script, so Grand Hyatt and W facts rest on news (SETN, ctee) and Booking.com room names.
  - The Grand Hotel's site returned an empty page.
- **Consensus.** Distinct editorial publishers in English and Traditional Chinese that name a hotel for watching the 101 fireworks from a room.
  - Excluded: booking sites and OTA content (Klook, KKday, Trip.com, Agoda, Booking), the hotels' own blogs (ILLUME's), coupon or deal aggregators (cpok.tw), job-site press reprints (1111.com.tw), TripAdvisor and forums.
  - The UDN group (udn travel + 經濟日報/money.udn) counts once. Nick Kembel (taiwanobsessed.com) counts once.
  - **marktrip.tw is flagged.** It is generic and affiliate-heavy, and our 101 research found its earlier list was a clone of mimigo. Its picks are shown in brackets and not counted.
- **Prices.**
  - **Booking.com, TWD, 2 adults, 1 room**, opened on 3 Oct 2026 in our own browser tab (non-essential cookies declined).
  - NYE = **Thu 31 Dec 2026, 1 night**. The control is **Thu 10 Dec 2026, 1 night**; Humble House used Wed 9 Dec because 10 Dec showed no availability.
  - Booking "alternative dates" figures are quoted where 31 Dec was unavailable.
  - Prices are a single-source snapshot.
- **Klook.** Every hotel URL below was either verified in an earlier research file (address checked) or opened by us on 3 Oct 2026 with its street address matched.
- **Working files** are in the session scratchpad `nye/`: raw HTML of every article and official page quoted, plus the extraction scripts.

## 1. Summary: what the writer needs to know

1. **NYE rooms now go on sale in late August and September, not mid-November.** All dates are 2026:

   | Hotel | On sale | Source |
   |---|---|---|
   | eslite hotel | 20 Aug | official |
   | Taipei Marriott | 2 Sep | official |
   | United Hotel | 7 Sep | official |
   | Humble House | 10 Sep | official, NOWnews |
   | Grand Hyatt | "September" | SETN |
   | Shangri-La | 24 Sep, 12:00 | udn/經濟日報, official |
   | Le Méridien | before 14 Sep | cpok, 14 Sep; official page live |

   - **How fast they are selling.** By **15 Sep 2026** ctee reported:
     - Grand Hyatt: fireworks rooms 60% sold, 31 Dec about 70% full.
     - Le Méridien: 80% of its 77 fireworks rooms booked.
     - Marriott: about 65%. W: about 50%.
     - Grand Hotel: about 90% of 150 fireworks rooms sold.
   - **The owner's fireworks page says mid-November in three places.** All three are wrong now (see §7).
2. **On Booking.com, every big view hotel had no availability for 31 Dec**: Grand Hyatt, W, Le Méridien, Humble House, Shangri-La, Marriott, eslite, Hanns House, Home Hotel, Pacific, Park Taipei, amba Songshan and Hotel Resonance. This was true for a 1-night search, and also for a 2-night search (31 Dec–2 Jan) at the Grand Hyatt, W and Le Méridien.
   - **The NYE nights are sold through the hotels' own 2-night packages.** The article should send readers to the hotel direct.
3. **Budget Xinyi hotels charge 9–11× on NYE.** Booking.com, 31 Dec vs Thu 10 Dec 2026:

   | Hotel | Room | 10 Dec (NT$) | 31 Dec (NT$) |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | Members Hotel | Standard Double | 3,380 | **36,800** |
   | Members Hotel | Classic Quadruple (balcony facing 101, per afterthirtytravel) | 4,680 | **64,800** |
   | CHECK inn | windowless Standard Double | 2,650 | **22,995** |
   | Formosa 101 | dorm bed (2-adult search) | n/a | 3,784 |
   | Formosa 101 | shared-bath double | n/a | 8,256 |
   | Eastin | (only room left) female single, shared bath | 1,360 | **7,000** |

   The owner's "NT$10,000–30,000 or more" understates the top end.
4. **Guaranteed 101 view vs "subject to actual view".**
   - **Guaranteed:** the Taipei Marriott (天際景觀客房, "保證101景觀房", 40 rooms) and the Grand Hyatt (101 View room categories, "guaranteed" per the Hyatt package snippet).
   - **Subject to the actual view** (依房型及實際視野為準): Le Méridien and United Hotel say so in their packages.
   - **Floors and facing:** Shangri-La's package rooms are on **15F–23F** (26F–33F by phone only). eslite allocates floors and facing on the day (city-view vs mountain-view).
5. **The wind matters for which side you are on.** Taipei 101's chair, quoted by Yahoo in Dec 2025, said that under the north-east monsoon the **east and north sides** give the more complete view.
   - **Our inference:** hotels west and south-west of the tower sit downwind on a typical NE-monsoon night: Shangri-La, Park Taipei, and Members, CHECK inn and Formosa 101 on Keelung Rd Sec 2.
   - Low-smoke Italian fireworks were used in 2025/26.
   - Not stated by any hotel. Present it as a tip, not a fact.
6. **Most NYE packages cannot be refunded.**
   - **Non-refundable once booked:** Humble House, Le Méridien and eslite (full prepayment). United also takes full prepayment; cancellation is under its booking terms.
   - **Shangri-La:** charges the 31 Dec night on 15 Dec, with free cancellation before 15 Dec.
   - **Marriott:** free cancellation until **30 Nov 23:59**, the most flexible.
7. **Single-night options do exist.**
   - **United Hotel** sells 31 Dec alone: NT$19,999 for an Executive 101 View room, tax and service included.
   - **Taipei Marriott** sells 31 Dec alone: NT$20,000 + 15.5% for a Sky View King with a guaranteed 101 view.
   - **Le Méridien's** terms say the package applies to "the night of 31 Dec, or two consecutive nights including 31 Dec", but every price is labelled "每房2晚" (per room for 2 nights). Ambiguous: see §6.
8. **Shangri-La's Marco Polo Lounge (38F) is closed for refurbishment**, reopening "mid-December" (official notice). The **43F rooftop pool closes at 15:00 on 31 Dec** for the hotel's party.
9. **Humble House's 6F restaurant and The Terrace bar are closed for an upgrade**, with no reopening date (official notice id=752). The NYE party is on 5F and in the 7F outdoor garden instead.
10. **Fireworks length:** 300 seconds officially (Taipei 101, Dec 2025), but CNA reported "6 minutes" on the night. "Five to six minutes" is safe.
11. **The fireworks have never been called off** in our searches, and 2025/26 went ahead in rain (CNA). The only COVID-era cancellation found (Dec 2020) was Taipei 101's **89F observatory party**, not the fireworks (Taiwan News).
12. **The 2026/27 show details have not been announced** as of 3 Oct 2026. Taipei 101 announced the 2025/26 details in December 2025. Travel.taipei lists "Taipei New Year's Party 2027" for 31 Dec 2026 to 1 Jan 2027 at City Hall Plaza, without times.

## 4. Per-hotel fact sheets

Notes for every sheet:
- Distances to 101 are **approximate straight-line estimates from map coordinates**, not measured. Walking times for Xinyi hotels are in the 101 research.
- "View" sides are as reported. The downwind note is our inference (see §1.5).
- Booking.com prices: Thu 10 Dec 2026 and Thu 31 Dec 2026, 2 adults, 1 night, TWD.

### 4.1 Grand Hyatt Taipei 台北君悅酒店 (Xinyi, next to 101)
- **Where:** 2 Songshou Rd, north-west of 101. Skybridge to the 101 mall. 850 rooms.
- **View rooms:**
  - Booking room names: "King/Twin Room with 101 View", "Premium King/Twin Studio with 101 View – Club Access", "Grand Executive Suite with 101 View".
  - Chinese package names (knews 2024): 君悅101景觀客房, 嘉賓軒101景觀客房, 嘉賓軒101景觀尊尚客房, 行政101景觀套房.
  - Only one side faces 101 (Kembel; Forbes: the east wing). About **190** fireworks rooms (ctee).
- **Guaranteed?** Yes, by category ("guaranteed Taipei 101-facing guestroom" in the Hyatt package, search snippet only).
- **How much you see:** right beside the tower, so you look up at it; Cathay says "unobstructed".
