## Pineapple cakes

**SunnyHills (微熱山丘)**: sunnyhills.com.tw/store/zh-tw/ (Northern Taiwan tab), opened 27 Oct 2026
- Taipei Minsheng Park shop: 台北市松山區民生東路五段36巷4弄1號1樓, 10:00-18:00, tel 02-2760-0508. Takes cash, cards, Line Pay.
- Directions: buses to 介壽國中 stop, then 3-5 min walk; about 13 min walk from MRT Songshan Airport station.
- Shop text invites visitors to sit with a cup of tea and a cake. Free cake and tea to visitors: taipeitourism.org/best-pineapple-cake-taipei/ (s).
- Other northern outlets on the same list: Taipei 101 mall To Go counter; Taoyuan Airport T2 To Go counter, T1 PopUp, T3 To Go counter.
- Prices, shop.sunnyhills.com.tw (opened): 6 for NT$345, 10 for NT$550, 15 for NT$825.

**Chia Te (佳德糕餅)**: chiate88.com.tw, opened 27 Oct 2026
- Address (105) 台北市南京東路5段88號, tel (02) 8787-8186. Shop hours on the site banner: 08:30-20:30. Founded 1975 (site header).
- Original pineapple cake: 6 for NT$228, 12 for NT$456, 20 for NT$760. Shelf life 40 days. Cranberry, egg-yolk and walnut versions: 15 days.
- Address check: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chia_Te_Bakery (s). Nanjing Sanmin station proximity: general knowledge, not separately checked.

**Taipei Leechi (台北犁記)**: no working official site (taipeileechi.com.tw does not resolve, 27 Oct 2026)
- Address 台北市中山區長安東路二段67號; hours "daily 08:00-21:00" (NOWnews, 10 Sep 2024, nownews.com/news/6521182) vs "09:00-21:00" (Supertaste, supertaste.tvbs.com.tw/food/350845). Copy says "roughly 08:00 or 09:00 to 21:00".
- Founded 1894 (NOWnews). Signature lines: egg-yolk pastry, pineapple cake, mung bean cake. Queues before Mid-Autumn and Lunar New Year (udn.com (s)).
- **No pineapple cake price found** (egg-yolk pastry about NT$65 each in 2024). The copy gives no price.

**Lee Tin Hsiang (李亭香)**: 迪化街一段309號, founded 1895; ping xi bing (平西餅) four for NT$220 (supertaste.tvbs.com.tw/food/357280 (s); thenewslens.com/article/151324 (s)). Hours conflict (09:00-20:00 vs 10:00-19:00), so none given.

## Tea

Copy: "widely said to date from 1883". Hours 07:00-21:00 from one third-party listing only, not used.

- Lishan wholesale NT$2,500-10,000 per jin (600 g): aboxtik.com/taiwan-expensive-tea/ (s). The "well over a thousand dollars for 150 g" line is our arithmetic from that range plus a retail mark-up, and is deliberately loose.
- Muzha Tieguanyin and Maokong: covered on the site's existing Maokong page; general knowledge.

## Kavalan
- Distillery in Yuanshan, Yilan; King Car Group; first spirit March 2006, first release December 2008 (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavalan_Distillery (s); thespiritsbusiness.com (s)).
- Kavalan Bar & Shop in Taipei 101, opened August 2021 (2022 report via search (s)). **Not reconfirmed as open in Oct 2026.**
- Distillery admission NT$200 from 1 March 2026 (taiwanderers.com (s)).
- Supermarkets (Carrefour) stock it: trvlogue.com/en/carrefour-taipei-shopping-guide (s).
- Eslite Wine Cellar Songyan (誠品酒窖松菸門市), 菸廠路88號, 11:00-22:00: Eslite store list (opened). Whether it stocks Kavalan is not confirmed, so the copy says "Taiwanese spirits" and "another place to look".

## Customs
- **Great Britain**: gov.uk/bringing-goods-into-uk-personal-use/arriving-in-Great-Britain (fetched): 42 L beer, 18 L wine, 4 L spirits over 22%, 9 L up to 22%, £390 other goods. gov.uk/bringing-food-animals-plants-into-uk (fetched): biscuits, bread, cakes without fresh cream, chocolate and confectionery may come from any country; restrictions on meat, dairy, fish and animal products.
- **US**: 19 CFR 148.33 (law.cornell.edu (s)): $800 exemption for returning residents; one litre of alcohol within it; declare all articles acquired abroad. APHIS: Taiwan ASF restrictions lifted 19 Aug 2026 but other pork restrictions (FMD, CSF, SVD) remain (aphis.usda.gov import alert (s)).
- **Australia**: agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/travelling/to-australia (s): declare all food, including commercially packaged snacks; declaring avoids penalty under the Biosecurity Act; infringement notices up to A$7,280 (amount not used in copy).
