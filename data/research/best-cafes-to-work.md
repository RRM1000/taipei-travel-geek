## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **Campus Café looks mostly closed in Taipei.** In search snippets, the official site gocampuscafe.com lists only a **Nanjing branch (南京店)** and a **Taipei Dome branch (台北大巨蛋店)** (s). ifoodie and OpenRice mark the **Neihu (基湖路) branch "已歇業" (closed)** (s). A food blog headline marks the **Zhongxiao (忠孝東路三段203號) branch "已歇業"** (s), but a Yelp page "Updated June 2026" still shows hours for it (s). Uber Eats shows the **Zhanqian (館前路36號2F) branch** off the platform since 15 Sep 2026 (s), which says nothing about the shop itself. **None of the three branches the page recommends can be confirmed open.** It is a diner rather than a work cafe anyway.
2. **The page's hero image shows a closed cafe.** The `best-cafes-to-work` featured image is `/media/2019/12/Out-of-Office-1024x658.jpg`. Out of Office is marked "Permanently Closed" on its own page (`out-of-office-cafe`). Apple Museum Cafe, also on the site, is closed too, yet George Lee's Threads list still recommends it (s).
3. **Cho Café is open but bad for laptops at weekends.** TVBS 食尚玩家's Ximending list (article 357115, date not visible; s) gives a **minimum spend of NT$150 on weekdays and NT$250 at weekends, a 1.5-hour limit at weekends, and closing on Wednesdays and Thursdays** (hours 11:00–19:00 on the other days). A 2019 WalkerLand review said "不限時" (no limit) (s), so the rules have tightened.
4. **Yaboo is no longer "no time limit".** A January 2026 article (s; publisher not identified in the snippet) says Yaboo **limits stays to 3 hours when it is full** ("不是不限時咖啡廳（限時3小時）"). Older reviews (WalkerLand: "我坐了7小時，沒人趕我") said no limit. It is **cash only**, takes **no reservations**, and has a **one-drink minimum** (s). City Nomads (~mid-2025) still recommends it for plugs and Wi-Fi.
5. **Café Costumice has weekend limits, a service charge and few plugs.** Snippets give **no limit on weekdays and 3 hours at weekends** (one newer post says 2 hours), a **10% service charge**, and a minimum spend (s). George Lee (2026) says the room is dark, **few seats have plugs**, and it feels more like a bar for chatting (s). The site's "four-way extension plugs on some tables" line is from 2020 and is not confirmed now.
6. **Youmoutoohana now has a better work branch.** 羊毛與花・華山 (Huashan, near Zhongxiao Xinsheng) is on TVBS's 2026 no-time-limit list as **no limit on weekdays and weekends, minimum one drink per person, no weekend bookings** (s). One Threads reviewer notes single seats with ramen-booth-style dividers (s). The Yongkang branch the site recommends shows no sign of closing. The brand site still lists it, and mymarcy and George Lee still recommend it (s).
7. **The cafes most recommended by Chinese-language publishers now are plug-at-every-seat, no-time-limit places.** None of them is on the current page: **Block & Cafe 町咖啡** (Main Station, open 08:00–01:00, every seat has a plug, paid printer), **Homey's cafe** (Zhongxiao Fuxing, plugs at every seat), **4F 劇場咖啡 Le Promenoir** (inside Zhongshan Hall by Ximen), **Arthere Café 上樓看看** (City Hall, Xinyi) and **Dreamers Coffee Roasters 南西店** (Zhongshan) (all s).
8. **English and Chinese publishers barely overlap.** The English lists (City Nomads, Gone Travelling, Half Half Travel, pebbles.day) favour specialty-coffee cafes (Miracle, TWATUTIA, Tian Ye, Roaster Family, Halfway). The Chinese lists favour cafes with an explicit 不限時 + 插座 policy. Only **Dreamers Coffee**, **Yaboo** and **TWATUTIA(N)** appear in both.
9. **"No time limit" usually comes with conditions.** The common patterns (s): one drink per person (most), a set minimum (Cho NT$150/250; Le Promenoir "250" in one source), "limited when full" (Yaboo 3 h, TWATUTIAN 90 min, CURISTA 2 h), weekend-only limits (Costumice, Cho, He Coffee 3 h, SunDay 2 h), and charging for sockets (嬌嬌咖啡 in Jingmei charges NT$20 for a plug).
10. **Louisa has no chain-wide time limit, but branch rules vary.** A CommonWealth (天下) interview describes no time limit, free plugs and Wi-Fi as company strategy (s, date not visible). Threads users report branch notices such as "leave your seat for over 20 (or 30) minutes and you must re-order / your things go to the counter" and bans on outside food (s). Half Half Travel (upd ~Sep 2026) says Louisa has **no private Wi-Fi network** (s). Treat Louisa as "check the branch".
11. **Wi-Fi is not a given at fashionable cafes.** Half Half Travel's own top pick, Gingin Coffee Company, now says on Instagram it has no Wi-Fi (s). Ke Fen Yuan has none (Gone Travelling, s), and UNI Café has no plugs (City Nomads, s).
