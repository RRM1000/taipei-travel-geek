# Research: Beitou Hot Spring Hotels

Checked 29 September 2026. This file is research, not article copy. Proposed slug: `beitou-hot-spring-hotels`.

The page is about where to **stay**. The public baths, day trips and bathing how-to already live in `taipei-hot-springs` and `xinbeitou`, so the new page should link to them rather than repeat them.

**How this was checked**
- **Two passes.**
  - Pass 1: a consensus count of English and Traditional Chinese editorial sources.
  - Pass 2: every hotel verified against its official site, plus Klook, Booking.com and Trip.com, and dated 2024–2026 reviews.
  - Pages were opened and read as text, not taken from search snippets. Anything from a snippet only is marked "(snippet)".
- **What was excluded from the counts.**
  - Booking engines and OTA content blogs: Klook, KKday, Agoda, Trip.com, AsiaYo, ezTravel, FunNow, Coolprice, triptripgo, corner.inc.
  - TripAdvisor, Wanderlog, forums (PTT, Dcard), social media.
  - welcometw.com 301-redirects to gofunit.com, so the two count once.
  - twosevenths.com is a mystery-shopper and affiliate hybrid, with "special price" buttons on every entry. It is shown as **borderline** and not added to the totals.
  - Single-hotel reviews (bunnyann, fupo, supertaste, Marie Claire, mimigo's Wellspring review, Taiwanderers' Gaia review) are used for verification, not for counts.
- **Prices.**
  - Booking.com, TWD, for **Wed 11 to Thu 12 Nov 2026, 2 adults, 1 room**, checked 29 Sep 2026. A Saturday check covered **Sat 14 to Sun 15 Nov**.
  - Hotels missing from Booking (Kagaya, Spring City) were priced on Klook. Klook would only show GBP, which was converted at £1 = NT$42.14 (open.er-api.com, 29 Sep 2026). Golden Hot Spring was priced on Trip.com in TWD.
  - Official package prices are given where they exist. These are snapshots, not editorial evidence.
- **Walking times** are the hotels' own figures unless marked. No Google Maps walking run was done this time (see Gaps).
- **Working notes** are in the session scratchpad `beitou/` folder: consensus.md, plus pages/*.txt dumps of the official pages.

## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **The Millennium Hot Spring (北投公園露天溫泉浴池, the NT$60 public open-air pool) has been shut since 24 Jan 2025.**
   - The parks office timetable (updated 29 Jul 2026) says the rebuild was due to finish on **26 Sep 2026**, with the reopening date "to be announced on the official site".
   - The owner's hot springs guide still describes it as open. Kembel (27 Sep 2026) says "closed until summer 2026", which is stale.
2. **Kagaya's green-sulphur annex (新秀閣青磺溫泉) closed permanently on 4 July 2025.**
   - Kagaya now offers **white sulphur only**.
   - afterthirtytravel (May 2026) still mentions a "blue sulphur sauna", and hk01 (Dec 2025) says Kagaya has "blue and white". Both are out of date.
3. **Villa 32 is the only hotel on the list with green sulphur (青磺)** as well as white. The official site says "白磺 and 青磺". Everything else is white sulphur.
4. **Hotel Royal Beitou is white sulphur, not green.** Bigfang's "青磺" is CORRECTED by the official room page: white sulphur from Sulphur Valley, pH 4–5.
5. **Spring City's big outdoor pools are swimsuit-and-cap, not nude.**
   - Its nude baths are in the members-only Red Cypress Club (18+). Hotel guests can use them once for NT$800 on the autumn–winter package.
   - A WebFetch summary had claimed the outdoor pools were nude. The official page says "使用此設施需著泳衣及泳帽".
6. **Spring City now costs about half Grand View's price, not a third.**
   - Weekday November: Spring City about NT$6,200 (Klook B&B) or NT$6,380 (official half-board for two).
   - Grand View: NT$12,600 (official weekday B&B) to NT$13,860 (Booking).
   - The owner's NT$3,800 for Spring City is out of date.
7. **Grand View's shuttle leaves from Beitou MRT (exit 1), per the official text.** The page also shows a Xinbeitou pick-up photo. Spring City's shuttle runs Beitou → Xinbeitou → hotel.
8. **Minimum ages for nude baths vary a lot.**
   - Villa 32: whole hotel 16+.
   - Grand View, Gaia and Asia Pacific: nude baths 12+.
   - Tian Yue Quan: 140 cm+. Sweetme: 120 cm+. Hotel Royal: 4+.
   - Spring City's outdoor swimsuit pools take all ages (under 110 cm free).
9. **No hotel publishes a tattoo rule.** Grand View, Kagaya, Gaia, Asia Pacific and Hotel Royal bath pages were all searched for 刺青/紋身/tattoo, with nothing found. The article should say this plainly, not promise either way.
10. **The Hot Spring Museum's hours are now 10:00–18:00, Tue–Sun** (last entry 17:45). The owner's Xinbeitou page says 09:00–17:00.
11. **"Minibus S15 from Beitou" is wrong** on two owner pages. S15 (小15) runs from Jiantan MRT to Qingtiangang. From Beitou MRT, **S9 (小9)** goes to Yangmingshan and Zhuzihu, and **bus 230** goes to Yangmingshan bus station.
12. **Waterhouse (享溫泉) warns that from October to March, check-in time is "set by the hotel on the day"** depending on demand. From April to September, Fri/Sat check-in is 18:00.
13. **Phoenix Pavilion has no lift.** Check-in is 17:00 on weekdays and 19:00 at weekends.
14. **Golden Hot Spring's and Chyuan Du's official sites were down** (HTTP 522) on 29 Sep 2026. Both are operating: Trip.com reviews are dated Sep 2026, and both were bookable for November.
15. **Smart Travel Asia still lists Whispering Pines (吟松閣).** That inn is widely reported closed since about 2014 (not re-verified; see Gaps). Don't use it.

### 4.1 Grand View Resort Beitou 北投麗禧溫泉酒店 (owner pick)
- **Address:** 30 Youya Rd (幽雅路30號). Tourist-hotel licence 交觀業字第1365號. Official site **gvrb.com.tw** (not grandviewresort.com.tw, which doesn't resolve).
- **Rooms:** 66, **all with a private balcony**. Sizes 50 m² (雅緻 rooms), 63 m² (幽雅 family and suites), 96–116 m² (麗禧 suites).
- **In-room bath:** yes, every room. The room page lists an "獨立冷熱分離泡湯池" made of Bali Guanyin-mountain stone, plus a hinoki bucket.
- **Spring:** **white sulphur**, piped from the Sulphur Valley (硫磺谷 / 大磺嘴) source about 1 km away, about 55°C at source.
- **Public baths:**
  - 露天風呂: nude, gender-separated, sides swap daily. 07:00–23:00, max 4 hrs. **No under-12s. No phones.** Non-guests NT$1,600 weekday / NT$1,800 weekend. Closed Monday nights 23:00–01:00 on the 1st and 3rd weeks.
  - 戶外風呂: mixed, **swimsuit and cap**. Outdoor pool plus two hot pools. 07:00–22:00. Adults NT$800; ages 6–11 NT$600. Under-12s must be with an adult. Closed Mondays 07:00–15:00.
  - Guests use both free.
- **Private rooms (day use):** 16 rooms. NT$2,500 / 90 min (semi-open-air, windowed or windowless), NT$3,000, NT$3,500 / 120 min. Minimum 2 people; +NT$600 per extra person 115 cm+. 24 hrs, book up to a month ahead.
  - Autumn–winter day packages (1 Oct 2026 – 30 Apr 2027): private room + lunch set for two NT$5,260; private room + afternoon tea NT$3,100.
- **Kids:** under 6 free (no breakfast). Ages 6–11: extra person NT$1,500+10%, extra bed NT$2,500+10%. 12+: NT$2,000/3,000+10%.
- **Food:**
  - 雍翠庭 (Taiwanese; Michelin-recommended per the hotel's 12 Jul 2026 news item) and a European restaurant (Le Cordon Bleu–trained chef per the hotel).
  - Breakfast "麗禧晨之光".
  - Half-board dinner NT$2,380+10% per person.
- **Check-in/out:** 15:00 / 11:00.
- **Getting there:** free shuttle from **Beitou MRT exit 1 (right side)**, first-come, no booking.
  - From the station: 10:00–18:00 on the hour and half-hour; 18:00–22:00 on the hour.
  - From the hotel: :15 and :45 from 09:45 to 17:45, then :45 from 18:45 to 21:45.
  - Also bus 小25 or 230 to 北投文物館.
- **Price:**
  - Official 冬日暖旅 B&B (1 Oct 2026 – 30 Apr 2027): 雅緻山景 **NT$12,600 weekday / NT$13,860 weekend**. Half-board NT$15,600 / 17,160. Weekday = Sun–Thu.
  - Booking Wed 11 Nov: Superior Twin **NT$13,860** with breakfast. Sat 14 Nov: only a Deluxe Quadruple at NT$24,926.
  - Rack rate for the 麗禧 suite: NT$45,000.
- **Owner's text:**
  - "luxury option on Youya Road": VERIFIED.
  - "large rooms with their own spring-fed baths": VERIFIED (50 m²+).
  - "family-friendly": VERIFIED with a caveat (nude baths 12+; the outdoor pool is fine for kids).
  - "shuttle": VERIFIED. Table "Xinbeitou + shuttle": the official shuttle text names **Beitou** MRT.
  - "NT$12,500": close to the official NT$12,600 weekday B&B.
- **Tattoos:** no rule stated.

### 4.2 Spring City Resort 北投春天酒店 (owner pick)
- **Address:** 18 Youya Rd (幽雅路18號).
- **In-room bath:** yes, all rooms ("客房內觀音石浴池", white sulphur). Klook's room tags only show "private hot spring" on Executive rooms, but the official site says all rooms have one.
- **Spring:** **white sulphur** ("帝王泉"), from the Dahuangzui source.
- **Public baths:**
  - **露天風呂:** nine outdoor spa pools, including a kids' splash pool, cold pool and hot pool. **Mixed; swimsuit and cap required.** 09:00–22:00, one entry, no time limit. Non-guests NT$800; children 110–140 cm NT$550; **under 110 cm free**. Free for guests.
  - **Nude baths:** only in the 紅檜俱樂部 (members' club, **18+**, closed the 2nd and 4th Mondays). On the autumn–winter package, guests get one entry for NT$800.
  - **Private rooms:** 室內湯屋, 24 hrs, 1 hr. NT$600 per adult, NT$400 per child 110–139 cm, under 110 cm free. Walk-in only, no phone booking.
- **Room list (rack):** 典雅雙人 NT$7,200+10%; 尊爵雙人 NT$7,800; 尊爵三人 NT$9,000; 景緻家庭 and 和式 NT$11,000; suites NT$12,000–25,000.
- **Kids:** one child without a bed per room. 110–140 cm NT$800; 140 cm+ NT$1,100. Children are defined by height.
- **Check-in/out:** 15:00 / 11:00.
- **Food:** 竹林亭 (Japanese kaiseki) and 幽雅18 (Chinese and Western).
- **Shuttle:** free. Hotel → Xinbeitou MRT → Beitou MRT, and back. Stops are at Beitou MRT exit 1 (right) and Xinbeitou MRT exit 1 (left). The timetable is an image (2024/1/1 version, still posted as the 2026 timetable).

  - Official 秋冬一泊二食 (1 Oct 2026 – 31 Mar 2027): **NT$5,800+10% for two (= NT$6,380)**, with breakfast and a NT$1,680 竹林 set dinner. **Fri/Sat/holidays +NT$2,000.**

### 4.3 Villa 32 三二行館
- **Address:** 32 Zhongshan Rd (中山路32號), beside Thermal Valley.
- **Recognition:** Relais & Châteaux; **MICHELIN One Key** (Key hotels article, 18 Sep 2026).
- **Rooms:** **5 only.** 松 and 櫻 are premium Japanese rooms; 碧 is a premium European room; 玉 and 晶 are deluxe European rooms. Each has its own hot spring bath.
