## 1. What surprised us

1. **Sun Yat-sen Memorial Hall will not reopen in late 2026.** The site's hall page and the old walk said "late 2026" / "Q4 2026". In October 2025 the hall's director, Wang Lan-sheng, said core works were slightly behind and that everything, including the auditorium stage equipment, would finish in **Q3 2027**, with the aim of hosting the Golden Bell and Golden Horse awards there in Q4 2027 (S3). The official site (S1) gives no reopening date; its only closure notice is still the 21 Feb 2024 one. Taipei Travel (S2, page updated 26 Oct 2026) still says "to the end of 2026" and shows the hall as "Closed". A Ministry of Culture document cited in search results gives a 22-month stage-equipment contract ending March 2027 (not opened; UNCONFIRMED). Conclusion: closed now; no reopening expected before 2027.
   - Supporting signal: the hall's 161st-birthday tribute on 12 Nov 2026 (09:00–11:00) is being held outdoors at the 中山碑林 in the park, not in the building (S1b).
   - **The site's `/sun-yat-sen-memorial-hall` page (title "Closed Until Late 2026") is now out of date** and should be updated separately.
2. **The Taipei 101 observatory ticket desk has moved.** Since 1 July 2026 it is temporarily on the **Office Tower 1F**, not the mall 5F (S6, S7). The old walk told readers to take the escalator to 5F for the entrance. The official ticket page now says "Please go to the Observatory Ticket Counter on the Office Tower 1F." The site's `/taipei-101` page does not mention the move.
3. **Official general ticket lists floors 89F and 88F** (S6). The site's taipei-101 page says NT$600 covers "88–91 including the open-air 91st floor"; the official page lists 91F only for group/field-trip tickets and warns 91F is weather-dependent. The draft says "88th and 89th floors" only.
4. **Songshan Cultural and Creative Park hours:** Taipei Travel lists the park as 08:00–22:00 every day (S4). The site's park page says "outdoor areas never close" and "indoor spaces 09:00–18:00"; neither figure could be confirmed on an official page (the park's own site gives directions but no hours; its brochure PDFs could not be read). The draft uses only 08:00–22:00.
5. **Eslite Spectrum Songyan** (S9): shops/2F stationery/3F kids 11:00–22:00; 3F bookshop and music store 24 hours; 3F eslite café 10:00–24:00 (weekend/holiday minimum spend NT$300 since 1 Oct 2025); B1 gallery Tue–Sat 11:00–19:00. B1 eating: 瀾之茶居 (tea house), 成真咖啡, 丸龜製麵 (Marugame udon, last orders 21:00). It is a handful of counters rather than a classic food court. Its 24-hour bookshop passed 10,000 days of non-stop opening on 28 Jul 2026 (S10).
6. **The Luminous Garden** (松菸夜光花園) light show runs in the Baroque Garden until 31 Dec 2026 (S5).
7. **MRT exits:** the hall's own page gives **exits 3 and 4** (S1c), so the old "exit 3" is right. The site's hall page recommends exit 4. The park's official directions use **exit 5** (S8).
