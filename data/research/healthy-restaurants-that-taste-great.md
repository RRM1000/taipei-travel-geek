# Research: Healthy Restaurants in Taipei That Still Taste Great

Checked 18 October 2026. This file is research, not article copy. Slug: `healthy-restaurants-that-taste-great` (existing page, last modified 13 Aug 2026; GSC 2,051 impressions, 3 clicks, avg position 8.7; top query "where to eat healthy", 763 impressions, 0 clicks, position 10.7). Companion file: `best-salads-in-taipei.md` (same date).

**Scope used**
- Sit-down or counter restaurants in Taipei City where the healthy angle is the point: grain/Buddha/market bowls, dietitian-led or calorie-labelled menus (健康餐), plant-forward and "clean eating" cafés, poke and 健康餐盒 lunch-box chains. Salad-first shops go to the salad page. Strictly vegetarian/vegan restaurants are on `best-vegetarian-restaurants-in-taipei`, and the aim is not to duplicate it.
- All four venues now on the page (Sugar Pea Cafe, Gonna, Miacucina, Tamed Fox) were re-checked, plus the site's related healthy pages (`quick-and-healthy-meals`, `yu-poke`, `haloa-poke`, `miss-energy`, `best-vegetarian-restaurants-in-taipei` and its venues).

**How this was checked (and the limits this time)**

Bash egress is blocked by the session proxy (403). **Everything below comes from search-result snippets, marked (s).** The search tool returns a model-written summary, so "attribution unclear" flags claims that may come from a different result than the one cited.
- **The search budget (200 per turn, shared across all agents) ran out** after about 45 queries for these two files. **Not searched:** Tamed Fox prices, Waypoint Santé address, Haloa Poke, Yu Poke, Miss Energy, Miss Green, Plants, BaganHood, Ooh Cha Cha status, Little Tree Food address. §6 lists what must be done before publishing.
- **Consensus rules** are the same as the salad file. - **Dates** given as "≈" were worked back from "N days ago" in the snippet. **MRT distances** are straight-line metres from the site's own map pins to approximate station coordinates. Exits and walking minutes were **not measured**.

## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **"Healthy" splits into three crowds that barely overlap.** English guides mean vegan/plant-forward cafés (Ooh Cha Cha, Plants, Miss Green, Sunami). Chinese lifestyle media mean 增肌減脂 calorie-counted food (鐵人伙房, Oma's deli deli, 一日樂食, Fancy Poké, 拾福青果). Brunch lists mean dietitian-led brunch (Tamed Fox, Sugar Pea). Only **Gonna** turns up in all three. The page's current mix (two brunch cafés, one vegetarian chain, one Mediterranean chain) only fits the brunch crowd.
2. **Tamed Fox was founded by a former dietitian and now has two branches.** Daan opened in 2017 and Xinyi (B1, Songren Rd, double the space, outdoor courtyard) in 2020; founder Debra is described as an ex-nutritionist building low-sugar, low-fat, high-fibre brunch (s). Our page and the list describe one location "away from everything".
3. **Our Tamed Fox page points to the wrong station.** It says "Closest MRT: Xinyi Anhe". The page's own map pin (Daan branch, 四維路76巷) is ~260 m from **Daan station** and ~700 m from Xinyi Anhe.
4. **Sugar Pea is open and is in Taipei Restaurant Week 2026.** It now trades as **"Sugar Pea Cafe & Kitchen"** (2026 臺北餐廳週 listing on flavornews; Linktree `sugarpea.taipei`). Yelp was updated July 2026 and has 4.8 from 11 reviews. A TripAdvisor review from May 2026 warns that there is often no walk-in space at lunch. It is the safest keep on the page.
5. **Miacucina's Neihu branch, the one the page calls "one of the nicest places we've dined in Taipei", is reported closed** (jjtheday.com post titled "2026（已歇業）… Miacucina 內湖店", single source). That sentence appears on this page, on `miacucina` and on `best-vegetarian-restaurants-in-taipei`. The Buddha bowls the page praises are still on the menu: Super Protein Buddha Bowl NT$473 (Uber Eats, Nanxi) and 綠巨人佛陀碗 NT$495 (Uber Eats, Fuxing).
6. **Our Miacucina "Shilin branch" points to the wrong station.** The page says Mingde exit 1. The Tianmu pin (德行西路48號, by Tianmu SOGO) is ~200 m from **Zhishan**.
7. **Gonna's "three branches" is now nine stores** (6 gonnaEAT + 2 gonnaEXPRESS + 1 gonna+, Aug 2025, s). Lion Travel aims for 12 more by about 2028. Gonna is the strongest salad pick, so it should move to the salad page.
8. **Several healthy places the site wrote up have closed.** The site already marks Herban Kitchen & Bar, Aone Eatery (Buddha bowls), Flourish, Amavie (acai), Mooo Salad, URBN Culture and Avenue Fast Casual as closed. Tricky Taipei's vegan guide (≈Feb 2022) still sends people to Flourish.
9. **The only healthy brand named by both Chinese and English sources besides Gonna is Waypoint Santé 鐵人伙房** (Taiwan Scene / Travel Taipei 2020; Bella ≈2022). Its founder is a triathlete and every dish is labelled with calories and macros (s). It isn't on the site at all.
10. **Poke chains are the fastest-growing "healthy" category in Chinese coverage.** JUKSY put **Fancy Poké** first of 10 healthy box shops (2024) and says it now has five stores in Xinyi, Da'an and Wenshan (2025). The site's poke pages (`yu-poke` and `haloa-poke`, the latter last edited Jan 2020) were not re-checked this time.
