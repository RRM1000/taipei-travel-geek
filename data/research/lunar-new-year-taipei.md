## 2. Dihua Street New Year market (台北年貨大街)

- **2026:** 31 Jan (Sat) – 15 Feb (Sun) 2026, the day before New Year's Eve (16 Feb). Dihua St Sec. 1 (Nanjing W Rd–Guisui St), Minsheng W Rd (Yanping N Rd–Huanhe N Rd), part of Guisui St (no stalls). Theme "奔年貨", with the Bailan Cat (白爛貓) IP. Business Today 30 Jan 2026 https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183030/post/202601300019/ (gives 08:00–24:00); 104 blog https://blog.104.com.tw/new-year-market/ (gives 12:00–24:00); another listing 11:00–24:00. **Hours conflict — article says "late morning to midnight" and hedges.**
- MRT: Beimen or Daqiaotou, about 8–10 min walk (Business Today).
- Origin: 1996, proposed by 迪化工作室 (GVM via search; not used in copy).
- **2027: not announced** (searched 27 Oct 2026; no Department of Commerce notice). Pattern: about two weeks, ending the day before New Year's Eve → expect roughly late January to Thu 4 Feb 2027. Hedged as an estimate.
- After it closes: Kembel (taiwanobsessed.com, 2026 guide) says Dihua goes quiet and shops close for several days over the holiday itself.
- Site already says (dadaocheng-dihua-street-walk): 2026 market 31 Jan–15 Feb; 2027 not announced. Consistent.

## 3. Lantern festivals

### Taipei Lantern Festival (台北燈節), run by the Taipei City Department of Information and Tourism (觀傳局)
- **2026:** 25 Feb – 15 Mar 2026, two zones: Ximen and Yuanshan (Expo Park). Lights from 17:00; weekdays 17:00–22:00, holidays 14:00–22:00 (PTS https://news.pts.org.tw/article/796012). Official site was 2026lanternfestival.travel.taipei. POP MART at Ximen, Transformers at Expo Park (newtalk/TVBS via search).
- **2025:** 2–16 Feb 2025, 17:00–22:00, four zones between Ximen and Beimen MRT stations, main lantern at Zhongshan Hall (Taiwan News https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/zh/news/6026241).
- **2024:** reported 2 Feb – 3 Mar, Ximen/Zhonghua Rd/Beimen (search summary only; not used in copy).
- **2027: not announced** (searches 27 Oct 2026). The dates move a lot year to year (before New Year in 2025, after it in 2026), so the article gives no estimate beyond "around the Lantern Festival".

### Taiwan Lantern Festival (台灣燈會, national)
- **2027 host: Miaoli County.** Tentative 20 Feb – 7 Mar 2027 (16 days); test lighting 17–18 Feb; single lantern area of about 30 ha in the Houlong HSR special district around **HSR Miaoli station**; theme 山海之間．慢城光苗. CNA 17 Mar 2026 https://www.cna.com.tw/news/ahel/202603170324.aspx. Hosts chosen by the Ministry of Transportation (Chiayi 2026, Miaoli 2027; Yahoo/CNA).
- 2026 was in Chiayi County (1111 news). 2023 was in Taipei (hero photo is from it; Commons file title).
- Status: **dates provisional.**

### Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival (平溪天燈節), New Taipei City Tourism and Travel Department
- **2026 (28th edition):** 27 Feb at Pingxi Junior High School, 3 Mar (Lantern Festival day) at Shifen Square. Nine waves each: 100 lanterns per wave at Pingxi, 150 at Shifen. Free release tickets: queue from 10:30 on the day at the service desk, or reserve online at skylantern.ntpc.gov.tw. Taiwan News https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/zh/news/6294512.
- Transport 2026: vehicle controls incl. scooters — 10:00–16:00 pass holders only, 16:00–23:00 no entry; Provincial Rd 106 closed in sections. TRA adjusted Pingxi/Shen'ao line timetables and added trains/cars on both dates. Shuttle buses from Taipei Zoo (Muzha), Ruifang, Keelung South and Shuangxi (fare table quoted from 2025). Tourist Shuttle 795 runs MRT Muzha–Pingxi/Shifen. NOWNEWS 26 Feb 2026 https://www.nownews.com/news/6789883; Business Weekly / udn via search.
- **2027: not announced** (searched 27 Oct 2026). Pattern: one or two nights close to the Lantern Festival (Sat 20 Feb 2027) → hedged.
- Klook: lantern experience products exist all year at Shifen (see §8).

## 4. What closes

- **Eve:** Kembel 2026 guide (https://www.taiwanobsessed.com/chinese-new-year-taipei/): most restaurants and bars shut on the Eve; Taipei 101 observatory shorter hours; CKS Memorial Hall no guard-changing; National Palace Museum, Children's Amusement Park, Maokong Gondola and Taipei Zoo listed as closed on the Eve; night markets officially open but many stalls closed; department stores shorter hours; 7-Eleven/FamilyMart open throughout. Days 2–3 shops and restaurants gradually reopen; some stay shut all week.
- **Children's Amusement Park 2026:** closed on the Eve; 14–21 Feb 09:00–20:00, 22 Feb 09:00–18:00 (search summary of Taipei Metro notice).
- **Taipei Zoo:** closes one day a year, on the Eve (2025 pattern: closed 28 Jan 2025, open 09:00–17:00 days 1–5). 2026 Eve not confirmed.
- **National Palace Museum:** in 2019 and 2024 it stayed open all holiday, closing at 16:00 on the Eve (Taiwan News; 2024 report). Kembel lists it closed on the 2026 Eve. **Conflict — article hedges: "check the museum's notice".** npm.gov.tw could not be opened (certificate error).
- **Hotel restaurants:** busy with reunion dinner sets (general; not quantified).
- **Longshan Temple:** guided tours suspended 14–22 Feb 2026 because of crowds (official notice 12 Feb 2026, lungshan.org.tw/tw/04_activity2.php?p=193). Normal hours 06:00–21:45 (site's own longshan-temple guide).
- **Xingtian Temple (official calendar, Feb 2026):** Eve open all night ("通宵開放不關廟門"); fortune slips, shoujing (收驚), jijie and family-fortune writing suspended from the Eve to day 5; services resume day 6. Also open all night on day 8 (eve of the Jade Emperor's birthday, day 9). In the run-up (lunar 12/24–12/28) jijie suspended and fortune slips end 20:00. Normal hours 04:00–21:00. https://www.ht.org.tw/ (calendar).

## 5. Getting around

- **MRT 2026:** holiday headways on all lines 14–22 Feb (about 2.5–9.5 min, about 12 min late at night; Xinbeitou/Xiaobitan branches 10–20 min); Zoo, Longshan Temple and Tamsui stations adjusted to crowds. NOWNEWS 14 Feb 2026 https://www.nownews.com/news/6784996. No overnight running reported for Lunar New Year's Eve (unlike 31 Dec, when Taipei Metro runs 42 hours non-stop — Taiwan News). Article says "we found no all-night service announced".
- **TRA 2026:** Spring Festival transport period 12–23 Feb. Reserved-seat booking opened 00:00 Tue 13 Jan (east), 00:00 Wed 14 Jan (west), 15 Jan (east real-name trains, Hualien/Taitung residents only) — about a month ahead. On the west line, EMU3000 free seating suspended; 250 same-day standing tickets per train, car 6 off-limits. People News 9 Jan 2026 https://www.peoplenews.tw/?p=11401.
- **HSR 2026:** transport period 13–23 Feb; booking opened Fri 16 Jan 2026 at midnight; 395 extra trains, 2,162 in total. Business Today 15 Jan 2026; NOWNEWS 14 Feb 2026. Early-bird fares on 617 trains (Manager Today, search only).
- **2027 booking dates: not announced** (searched 27 Oct 2026). Pattern: about four weeks before the holiday (13–16 Jan for a 14 Feb start in 2026) → expect early January 2027. Hedged.
- **Holiday travel pattern:** outbound rush before the Eve, return rush on the last days (general; TRA/HSR add northbound trains: 225 of 395 HSR extras were northbound in 2026).

## 6. Temples and customs

- **Longshan:** visitor incense stopped March 2020 (site's own guide); online and on-site lamp registration (光明燈, 安太歲); 2026 on-site lamp registration opened 15 Dec 2025 (official notice 17 Nov 2025, p=188); lantern-magnet souvenirs sold out before the 2026 New Year (p=192); held a Lantern Festival lantern-themed tour on 9 Mar 2026 (p=195), so the lantern displays stay up after the 15th day. Busy around midnight on the Eve and on day 1 (Kembel; general).
- **Taipei Xiahai City God Temple (Dadaocheng):** built 1859; Yue Lao (月下老人); first-time visitors buy a set of offerings (incense, paper money, red thread, sweets) at the counter; only blood relatives may pray on someone's behalf; couples return with wedding cakes (6,000+ a year, per the temple via TVBS search). Holiday hours not published online (temple site tpecitygod.org failed with a certificate error). Not the same as **Songshan Xiahai City God Temple** (Bade Rd Sec. 4), whose day-5 wealth-money handout drew a 1–2 km queue on 21 Feb 2026, 20,000 slips (PTS https://news.pts.org.tw/article/795787; NOWNEWS https://www.nownews.com/news/6787846).
- **Xingtian:** see §4. Known for having removed incense burners (2014; general knowledge, consistent with the site's guidance) — article doesn't claim a date.
- **Etiquette:** modest dress, keep to the side of worshippers, no flash near people praying, cushions are for kneeling (site's Longshan guide, paraphrased in new words).

## 7. Red envelopes and food

- **Red envelopes (紅包):** given after the reunion dinner; elders give to children, working adults give to parents and grandparents. Even amounts; avoid 4; new notes; don't seal. Common amounts in 2026: NT$600, 1,200, 2,200, 3,600. Taiwan News 9 Feb 2026 https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6299003. Don't fold the envelope; for elders, increase each year (TVBS/udn via search).
- **Food:** whole fish, left partly uneaten (年年有餘, "surplus every year"); long-year greens (長年菜, mustard greens cooked uncut); radish cake (菜頭 sounds like 好彩頭, "good omen"); fa gao (發糕, the wider the cracks, the better the luck). TVBS Health https://health.tvbs.com.tw/nutrition/352574; udn https://udn.com/news/story/123907/9277604.
- **Reunion dinner (圍爐):** family meal on the Eve (Taiwan News https://www.taiwannews.com.tw/news/6025001).
- **Dihua goods:** sweets, nuts, dried seafood, mullet roe, sausages (site's own Dihua guide).
