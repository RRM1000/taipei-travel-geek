# Research: Xinbeitou Hot Springs Walk

Checked 27 October 2026. This file is research, not article copy. Slug: `xinbeitou-hot-springs-walk`.

Route: Xinbeitou MRT → Beitou Park → Beitou Hot Spring Museum → Beitou Library → Thermal Valley. Optional extras: Ketagalan Culture Center, Longnaitang, Plum Garden, Puji Temple. The Millennium Hot Spring is pinned as closed, not as a stop.

Builds on `data/research/beitou-hot-spring-hotels.md` (checked 29 Sep 2026). Nothing here contradicts it, but two things have changed since then:
- The museum announced (on 29 Sep 2026) a full closure from 9 Nov 2026.
- Taipei Travel has taken down its Millennium listing.

---

## 1. What surprised us

1. **The Beitou Hot Spring Museum closes from 9 November 2026 to about October 2027** for a restoration and reuse project (修復再利用工程). Reopening is "expected in late October 2027, exact date to be announced". None of the site's pages mention this yet. Seven pages send readers there (see section 5).
2. **The Millennium Hot Spring has still not reopened.**
   - Press coverage gave a 28 July 2026 completion date (TVBS, ETtoday). The parks-office timetable later said 26 Sep 2026 (hotels research).
   - No reopening announcement could be found on 27 Oct 2026.
   - Taipei Travel's English listing (`/en/attraction/details/713`, "Temporarily Closed") now redirects to a 404 page.
   - Treat it as closed (status UNCONFIRMED beyond "no reopening announced").
3. **Thermal Valley is 80–100°C, not "60°c to almost 100°c"** (official Taipei Travel text). The xinbeitou page understates the low end.
4. **The library closes on the first Thursday of each month**, on top of national holidays. The TPML closed-dates list for 2026 includes 5 Nov, 28 Nov (Election Day) and 3 Dec.
5. **Mondays are a poor day.** The museum, Thermal Valley, the Plum Garden and the Ketagalan Culture Center all close. Only the park and the library (09:00–17:00) are open.
6. **The museum runs free Mandarin tours Tue–Fri at 11:00, 14:00 and 16:00** (about 30 min, none at weekends). This only applies until 8 Nov 2026.
7. **The xinbeitou page says Xinbeitou MRT "exit 4"** in the museum section but "exit 1" in its location box. Every 2026 source found refers to exit 1. The station's exit list could not be opened (UNCONFIRMED). Recommend dropping "exit 4".

- **Museum on Taipei Travel** (S11) still shows normal hours. Trust the museum's own notice (S2).
- **Xinbeitou MRT exits:** not confirmed from Taipei Metro.
- **Plum Garden and Ketagalan admission:** no fee is stated on Taipei Travel. The draft gives hours only.
- **Where to eat:** no individual restaurant was verified, so the draft stays general. Kahu Craft Beer Garden (on `best-outdoor-drinking`) and 滿來/滿足 ramen near the station couldn't be confirmed as operating.
- **Hotel day-use prices** come from the 29 Sep 2026 research and weren't re-checked.
- **Distance** is an estimate until `scripts/build-walks.mjs` routes the line. The input file here has no path.
