## Errors found in existing stop pages

Each find string below occurs exactly once in `content/posts.json` (checked by counting raw occurrences on 2026-10-27).

1. **`michelin-food-stands-at-night-markets`, Ningxia hours.** The page gives 17:00–01:00; the city's official listing (S8, updated 2026-10-27) gives 17:00–23:30 daily, with individual stalls setting their own hours.
   - Find: `Open <strong>17:00</strong> &ndash; <strong>01:00</strong>`
   - Replace: `Open <strong>17:00</strong> &ndash; <strong>23:30</strong>`

No other factual errors were found in the stop pages checked: `/dihua-street-dadaocheng-guide` (temple 1859, 600+ deities, Yue Lao, PIER5, Beimen exit 3, Daqiaotou exit 1), `/ningxia-night-market` (Shuanglian exit 1, Zhongshan exit 5), `/ximending` North Gate section (1884, 1966 restyling, protected site), `/best-temples-in-taipei#xiahai`. "Built in 1859" on the Dihua and temples pages is acceptable: the temple's own history says building started in 1856 and finished in 1859.
