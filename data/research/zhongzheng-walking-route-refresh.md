## Errors found on existing stop pages (not edited; for the owner)

1. **/presidential-office-building**: the intro says "partially open to the public on weekend mornings". The official schedule is weekday mornings, Mon–Fri 09:00–12:00 (S1). The Opening Hours section on the same page already says Monday to Friday, so the intro contradicts it. Its "partially open means" aside could be answered: on weekdays only the ground-floor exhibitions and corridors are open.
2. **/presidential-office-building** and **/taipei-guest-house**: "open on the same dates". They only partly overlap (S1, S2).
3. **/taipei-guest-house**: the "Opening Hours" heading has no content under it. Suggest: selected Saturdays 09:00–16:00, last entry 15:30, dates on the MOFA site (S2).
4. **/peace-park**: "Taipei 228 Memorial Hall NT$20 / Free for those under 12 or over 65". Entry is now free (S9).
5. **/jin-feng-braised-pork-rice**: "Long opening times (08:00 to 01:00) every day". Three listings give 11:00 opening and Monday closing (S12–S14).
6. **/national-taiwan-museum**: the opening times omit that ticket sales stop at 16:30. Over-65s are free on weekdays and pay half price at weekends, not half price at both (S4). The Land Bank building is now officially the 古生物館 / Paleontology Hall.
7. **/chiang-kai-shek-memorial-hall**: the hall and guard details agree with S8. No change needed.
