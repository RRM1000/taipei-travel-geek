## Map-data problems to report (not changed)

1. **East stop 5 (Indigenous Cultural Centre) no longer exists.** The building (原民風味館) stopped operating on 30 April 2022 and was demolished in 2023 for the TFAM extension. The post keeps the stop number, describes it as the site of the former centre, and moves on to the floral tunnel. The owner may want to rename the stop in the map data (for example "Site of the Indigenous Cultural Centre") or drop it and renumber.
2. **East stop 1** sits about 55 m north of the OSM node for exit 1. Harmless at walking scale.
3. The audit's note still stands: the **My Maps layer names are swapped** (the East route is in the layer called "West Walk", and the reverse). The site's map data uses the right labels.
4. The route `duration` values ("5 to 6 hours", "3 to 4 hours") were kept as the owner's estimates. With the Indigenous Cultural Centre gone the East route may take a little less time.

## Errors spotted on other pages (not edited)

- **/dalongdong-baoan-temple-and-gardens**: opening hours (closed Mondays, Sunday to 17:00) disagree with travel.taipei's daily 06:00–21:00 (S17).
- **/taipei-story-house**: still uses the old name, hours (10:00–17:30) and "closed during national holidays"; it is now Maison ACME (S6–S8).
- **/maji-square**: mentions craft shops and a deli supermarket; the operator has dropped retail (S24). The bar and restaurant list may also be out of date.
- **/xinsheng-park**: "over 700 varieties"; the 2026 press says over 800 (S15).
- **/lin-an-tai-ancestral-house**: "Closed on Mondays and during national holidays"; the official list is Mondays plus four festivals (S13).
- **/confucius-temple**: "4D theatre runs films from 10:00 to 16:00 daily" and archery could not be confirmed.
- **/expo-farmers-market**: links to expofarmersmarket.gov.taipei, which did not resolve on 27 Oct 2026.
