Checked 28 September 2026. This file is research, not article copy. The article is written by `scripts/add-hotels-near-ximending.mjs`.

- **Two passes, as for the Main Station guide.**

## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **Ximen WOW Hostel has closed.** It had 3 English hostel round-ups, but its address (8F, No. 42 Hanzhong St) is now registered as 喵喵有約青年旅社 / Meow Day Hostel (register licence 482). Hostel Geeks (May 2026), Road Affair and Nomadic Mick still list WOW, which is stale. It was dropped.
2. **VIA Hotel Ximen has also closed.** The official FAQ page is titled "Ximen (Closed)". It was never a candidate.
3. **Oani (綠洲), a new MEANDER-group hostel, opened on 8 Dec 2025** beside exit 3.
   - It is the most expensive hostel in the guide: a NT$1,200 bunk on a weekday rises to NT$3,600 on a Saturday.
   - Kembel's "same building as CityInn Plus" is loose. The addresses are 69 and 63 Baoqing Rd, on the same block.
4. **The owner's Cho Hotel paragraph is wrong in several places.** Cho 1 and Cho 3 are across the street from each other, not next door. Neither sells singles. Only Cho 1 has quads. The "Three" Klook link on the where-to-stay page points to Cho 1's page.
5. **Cho Hotel, Roaders Zhonghua and Roaders Plus (Taipei Station) are all Fawn Group (小鹿文娛) brands.**
6. **Papa Whale is essentially all windowless.** Woohotel (Jan 2023) marks every room type 沒有窗戶. There are 335 rooms (register), not 365. Its official walk is 10 min from exit 6.
7. **Energy Inn has no single rooms.** Its cheapest room is a 12 m² windowless double.
8. **Sotetsu Grand Fresa posted a construction-noise notice** (26 Jan 2025) for rooms facing a new building next door. The rooms page still links it in Sep 2026.
9. **amba's child policy** is 2 children aged 12 or under free, not "under 5" as in blogs.
10. **Westgate was the dearest hotel on the check date.** Only Premier rooms were on sale, at NT$8,370. Its cheaper Cozy room (26 m²) is officially windowless.
11. **Exit facts.** Exit 4 is Hengyang Rd, not "towards Main Station". Exit 5 (Zhongshan Hall) is the northern one, reached through the Ximen underground mall, which reopened on 20 Jan 2026 as UNDERCITY: XIMEN.
12. **Airport bus.** Kuo-Kuang 1819 does *not* stop at Ximen. Airbus 1961 does.
13. **Buskers.** Since 16 Sep 2024, busker amplification in the pedestrian zone is banned after 21:00.

### 4.1 Sotetsu Grand Fresa Taipei Ximen 相鐵GRAND FRESA 台北西門
- **Address:** No. 57, Sec. 1, Zhonghua Rd (east side). Register hohi_id 34470.
- **Opened:** 2 Feb 2024 (PR Newswire). Sotetsu's first hotel in Taiwan.
- **Rooms:** 200 (132 doubles, 68 twins). All rooms 30 m²+ (official), from 30.7 m² (Ximen Double) to 55.8 m² (Grand Twin, sleeps 4).
- **Bathrooms:** shower-only in standard rooms. Bathtubs in the family rooms only (sisy1017, iTravelBlog).
- **Family-room extras:** washer-dryer, microwave and fridge (two blogs, 2024–25; not on the official list, so **not used**).
- **Walk:** exit 3, 1 min (GM); official "about 1 min from exit 3". "Exit 6" in some blogs is wrong: it is across Zhonghua Rd.
- **Price:** Standard Double (No View) **NT$6,930**; Sat NT$11,286.
- **Kids:** under 12 free on existing bedding (official FAQ).
- **Check-in/out:** 15:00 / 11:00. 24h desk plus self check-in kiosks.
- **Facilities:** paid laundry on 3F. No hotel car park (a public paid lot is in the building). Breakfast depends on the rate.
- **Noise:**
  - Official notice 26 Jan 2025: daytime construction noise from the adjacent plot, from April 2025. It affects Standard Double/Twin, Deluxe Family and Connecting rooms.
  - Jieyi (May 2024 stay): noise only at weekends on low floors, and low-floor rooms can be seen into.
- **Status of the works:** whether construction is still running is unconfirmed (see Gaps).

### 4.2 Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Taipei Ximen 索拉利亞西鐵飯店台北西門
- **Address:** 6F, No. 88, Sec. 1, Zhonghua Rd (entrance on Hankou St Sec. 2). Register hohi_id 33655.
- **Opened:** Aug 2023. Nishitetsu's first hotel in Taiwan.
- **Rooms:** 298 (register). The hotel occupies 1F and 6F–28F; lobby 6F, breakfast 7F.
- **Room sizes:** standard rooms 21 m², in shower or bathtub versions; Comfort Twin 24 m²; Corner Twin 28 m²; Deluxe Twin 33 m²; Triple 39 m².
- **Views:** north-facing rooms look over the Tamsui River (kenalice).
- **Walk:** exit 6, 5 min (GM); blogs 4–5 min.
- **Price:** Comfort Twin **NT$7,326**, Queen NT$7,608.
- **Kids:** children of any age welcome (vivianexplore). No official free-age rule found.
- **Check-in/out:** 15:00 / 11:00.
- **Facilities:** gym; paid coin laundry (register); breakfast sold separately on some rates. No parking.
- **Noise:** consistently described as quiet or well soundproofed (acisni, Away to the City, Travel Lemming, andyventure).
- **Size opinion:** Kembel says the rooms are small for the price (snippet). Entry rooms are 21 m², against 30 m²+ across the road.
