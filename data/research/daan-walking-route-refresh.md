## Closures

Nothing on the route has closed. The one material change is that the original Din Tai Fung (Xinyi branch) no longer has seating.

## Map-stop problems (not changed, for the map owner)

- **Stop 4** is named "Yongkang Street", but the coordinates (25.034439, 121.526648) are Dongmen Market, about 45 m from the OSM market node. That is roughly 330 m north-west of the top of Yongkang Street at Xinyi Rd (25.0335, 121.5297) and about 350 m from Yongkang Park. The walk text now says that marker 4 is at Dongmen Market. Either rename stop 4 to something like "Yongkang Street & Dongmen Market" or move it to Yongkang Park (25.031766, 121.529804).
- **Stop 2** "Jianguo Weekend Artist's Corner" has no page and no current source. It is at the Xinyi Rd end of the flower market, which is correct for the market's south end.
- **Stop 1** is at the Ren'ai Rd end of the flower market (the north entrance). The jade market is a little further north (25.0396).
- **Stop 6** has no name issue, but it is exit 2 specifically. "Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall MRT (exit 2)" would be clearer.
- Leg 5→6 (1,648 m) loops through Liberty Square to the gate and back south to exit 2, which is why the line is much longer than the straight-line distance.
