# Research: Hotels near Taoyuan Airport

Checked 29 September 2026. This file is research, not article copy. Proposed slug: `hotels-near-taoyuan-airport`.

**Scope used**
- Beds inside or joined to the terminals (CHO Stay capsule hotel, the airside Plaza Premium lounge nap suites, and free sleeping spots).
- Hotels by Airport MRT stations: A14a Airport Hotel, A15 Dayuan, A11 Kengkou, A8 Chang Gung Hospital, A9 Linkou, A17/A18/A19 (Qingpu, the HSR Taoyuan area).
- Dayuan hotels with their own shuttles (City Suites Gateway, Holiday Inn, Sheraton, Bluewater).
- A short comparison with staying at Taipei Main Station (existing page `hotels-near-taipei-main-station`).
- **Nothing suitable was found at A10 Shanbi or A21 Huanbei.** No Nankan hotel with a *free* airport shuttle was found (see 4.14 and section 7).

**How this was checked**
- **Consensus.** English and Traditional Chinese editorial pages were opened with WebFetch or the browser, not read from snippets. "(s)" = seen as a search result title or snippet only, not opened.
- **Excluded from counts:** booking engines and OTA content (Booking, Agoda, Trip.com/hk.trip.com guides, Klook, KKday blog, FunTime blog, ezTravel, colatour, Hotels.com, Expedia, Wing On), TripAdvisor, FlyerTalk, Wanderlog, forums, social media, press releases. **taipeihorizon.com was excluded** as an AI/content-farm list (it still recommends Novotel alongside Hyatt, and lists an "Evergreen Transit Hotel within airport" that doesn't exist). **Taiwan Meanderings** (23 Mar 2026) is shown separately in brackets: it's anonymous and affiliate-heavy.
- **One publisher, one count:** travel.udn.com and woman.udn.com are both udn (United Daily News group) and count once. Nick Kembel's Taiwan Obsessed counts once.
- **Train times and fares:** Taoyuan Metro (tymetro.com.tw) station timetables (A1, A3, A8, A10–A15, A18, A21, A22) and the official journey planner (index_search.php), queried 29 Sep 2026 for a weekday. Raw summaries in scratchpad `tpe/`.
- **Prices:** Booking.com, TWD, **Wednesday 11 to Thursday 12 November 2026, 1 night, 2 adults** (1 adult for dorm/capsule beds), cheapest rate on sale (can be non-refundable), checked 29 Sep 2026. Saturday = **14 to 15 November**. If sold out, Sat 7 or 21 Nov was tried and is labelled. Single-source snapshot, not editorial evidence.
- **Walk times** are official or from reviewers. **No Google Maps walks were run** for this page (gap, section 8).

## 1. Summary: what surprised us

1. **Novotel no longer exists.** The Novotel Taipei Taoyuan International Airport (華航諾富特) became **Hyatt Regency Taoyuan International Airport (桃園國際機場凱悅酒店) on 1 January 2025**. It is still China Airlines-owned and it is still the airport's official "機場旅館" (taoyuan-airport.com, dated 21 Jan 2025). Several 2026 blogs (bobbytravel Aug 2026) still call it Novotel.
2. **There is no airside transit hotel at Taoyuan.** The only bed inside a terminal is **CHO Stay (町·草休行館), T2 5F south, landside**. Its official FAQ says arriving and transit passengers **must clear immigration** to reach it, and departing passengers should check in there *before* going through security. The only airside beds are the paid "private relaxation suites" in the **Plaza Premium Lounge (now "The Terroir"), T2 Zone A**, which needs a boarding pass.
3. **CHO Stay no longer sells hourly rests.** Official FAQ: "目前無提供休息方案預約". Blogs say hourly rests ended around Nov 2023. The shin.tw 2026 guide (and a trip.com-style snippet) still says "hourly", which is out of date. Check-in 16:00, check-out 10:00; if you arrive **04:00–07:00**, book the *previous* night and get free late check-out to 16:00.
4. **The landside T1–T2 skytrain stopped on 1 July 2026** (Terminal 3 works). Between terminals landside you now use the Airport MRT (**free between A12, A13 and A14a** with an EasyCard/iPass/icash, credit card or mobile pay) or a **free 24-hour shuttle bus** (every 15 min 06:00–22:59, every 20 min 23:00–05:59). The airside skytrain still runs. This matters for CHO Stay guests flying from T1, and for Hyatt guests.
5. **The Airport MRT is free between T1, T2 and the Hyatt's A14a station** (same rule, udn 2026 and Taoyuan Metro promotion since 2017). The list fare is NT$25.
6. **The Sheraton Taoyuan's shuttle is now useless for early flights.** Official 2026 timetable (effective 15 Feb 2026): airport **drop-off only**, at **07:00, 11:00, 14:00 and 16:00**, booked the day before. **No airport pick-ups** any more.
7. **Holiday Inn Taoyuan Airport by IHG is the old Hotel Orchard Park New Wing (桃禧航空城酒店-新館)**, rebranded 2025 (OTA snippets). It is the Sheraton's sister (the Sheraton's footer lists it as a group company). IHG's own page: "one-way shuttle to Taoyuan airport and HSR station by reservation", 250 rooms, check-in 15:00, **minimum check-in age 18**. The IHG facts panel also says "Airport shuttle: not available", which conflicts. Reported departures are 04:50, 05:50, 06:50, 08:50 and 10:50 (snippet, not verified).
8. **The Sheraton Taoyuan is the old Hotel Orchard Park (opened 2010), rebranded 22 Sep 2019** (Wikipedia). Its Booking URL is still `taoyuan-orchard-park`. Some ZH blogs (ajtravel) muddle the names.
9. **Backpackers' Hostel Taoyuan Airport has suspended its shuttles** ("由於人力不足，暫停接駁服務"). Only double-room guests get one free pick-up from A11 Kengkou. There is **no 24-hour desk** (check-in 16:00–23:30), and it had **no Booking.com availability on any November date tried**.
10. **Bluewater (藍水輕旅) gives free airport drop-offs, bookable until 23:00 on the day of check-in**, and has a 24-hour desk with no latest check-in time. At NT$1,399 (single, shared bath) it is the best-value option with a real early-morning transfer. One blog says "weekdays"; the official site doesn't say so (conflict).
11. **alfar Hotel (A17) is adults-only** (Booking). Don't put it in a family section.
12. **Airport taxis cost more than the owner's post says.** The official airport taxi fleet's estimate to **Taipei City is NT$1,200–1,900**, metered, plus tolls. The owner's `taoyuan-airport-mrt` post says NT$1,000–1,200. The airport also runs a **taxi-share service 00:00–06:00**.
13. **First trains don't help very early flights.** The first MRT reaching T1 from Taipei arrives 06:06. From A8 the first express reaches T1 at 06:06. From A18 the first train (05:47) reaches T2 at about 06:03. **Only the Hyatt (shuttle from 04:00, every 30 min), Holiday Inn (04:50, booked) and Bluewater (drop-off booked the night before) get you there before 06:00 without a taxi.** CHO Stay is in the terminal.
14. **Terminal 3:** only the **North Concourse** has opened: trial from 1 Dec 2025, full operation **25 Dec 2025**, 8 gates (D11–D18 per Taiwan News snippet). The main T3 building and south concourse are due in **2027**. You check in at T2.
15. **Kuo-Kuang 1819 moved in Taipei on 1 Jan 2025**: it now boards at **Taipei Bus Station (台北轉運站) 4F, platform 409**, not the old Kuo-Kuang terminal. Still 24 hours.

## 4. Per-option fact sheets

Prices are Booking.com TWD, 2 adults, Wed 11 / Sat 14 Nov 2026, cheapest rate on sale, unless stated.

### 4.1 CHO Stay Capsule Hotel 町·草休行館 (inside T2, landside)
- **Address:** South side, 5F, No. 9 Hangzhan S Rd (inside Terminal 2). Taoyuan hotel licence no. 263. Tel +886 3 383 4683.
- **Getting there (official):** go to T2 **3F departures hall south side, check-in counter 22**, take the escalator to 5F; it's on the left.
  - **Landside.** Arriving or transit passengers **must clear immigration** first (so you need to be able to enter Taiwan). Departing passengers should *not* go through security first.
  - Flying from **T1**: free MRT A12↔A13 or the 24-h shuttle bus (the landside skytrain stopped on 1 Jul 2026).
  - Vivianexplore's summary says "airside only", but that contradicts the official FAQ. Use the FAQ.
- **Rooms (official FAQ):**
  - "Single capsule" beds in **8–14-bed dorms, men and women separate**.
  - Private **double, triple and quad** rooms (mixed sex allowed).
  - **All bathrooms are shared**: showers, Dyson hairdryers, 茶籽堂 shampoo and body wash. Towel for hire; toothbrush kits for sale.
  - Booking lists 9 "rooms" (room types).
- **Check-in/out:** **16:00 / 10:00**. The front desk is 24 h (Booking/Klook). **Arriving 04:00–07:00:** book the previous night and note your arrival time; free late check-out until 16:00. Room cleaning 09:00–16:00 "may be noisy".
- **Day use:** **none**. "目前無提供休息方案預約". Hourly rests (formerly NT$500 for 2 h + NT$150/h) ended around Nov 2023 (snippet).
- **Kids:** **4+ only**; children can't share a single capsule. One child aged 4–6 stays free in a double/triple/quad with a double bed.
- **Food:** none served; no eating in rooms. FamilyMart and 7-Eleven (24 h) are on the same floor.

## 5. Comparison: stay by the airport or at Taipei Main Station?

For the short comparison section (link `hotels-near-taipei-main-station`).

| Your flight | Best base | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Departs **before about 08:30** | Airport (Hyatt, CHO, Holiday Inn or Bluewater drop-off) | The first express from A1 is **05:30** (T1 06:06, T2 06:09). With 2–3 h check-in advice, 06:06 at T1 only works for flights from about 08:30. The Hyatt's shuttle starts at 04:00 |
| Lands **after about 22:30** | Airport, or CHO if you're flying on | The last express to Taipei leaves T1 22:58 / T2 22:55; the last train leaves T1 **23:37**. After that it's the 1819 bus or a NT$1,200–1,900 taxi |
| Departs mid-morning or later, from 09:00 | Taipei Main | A1 in-town check-in 06:00–21:30 (China Airlines, Mandarin, EVA, Uni Air, Cathay, STARLUX), at least 3 h before. The express takes 36/39 min |
| Layover of 8+ h with a visa-free entry | Taipei Main | 36–39 min each way, NT$160; left luggage at the airport NT$320+/day or lockers NT$40–80/3 h |
| Layover of under about 6 h, or no right to enter | Airside: T1 4F 24-h zone, T2 3F recliners, or the Plaza Premium suites | CHO is landside |

Main Station page facts (already verified in `hotels-near-taipei-main-station-VERIFIED.md`):
- Roaders Plus 4 min from A1.
- Star Hostel 5 min (desk 07:00–23:00, then self check-in).
- Palais de Chine 6 min, with a free shuttle from A1 11:00–16:00.
- citizenM 5–10 min.
- A1 to the Metro lines is 10–15 min on foot.
